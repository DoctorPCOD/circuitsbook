    Phase and Root Mean Square

    1.

    Calculate the RMS value of the magnitude of the voltage and current signals, as well as the phase difference between the two signals.
    🔗
    \begin{align*} v(t) \amp= 120\cos(2\pi60t+120\pi/180)~\textrm{V}\\ i(t) \amp= 5\cos(2\pi60t+60\pi/180)~\textrm{A} \end{align*}
    🔗
    Answer.
    \begin{align*} |\vec{V_{RMS}}| \amp= 84.9~\textrm{VRMS}\\ |\vec{I_{RMS}}| \amp= 3.54~\textrm{ARMS}\\ \theta \amp= 60^\circ \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗
    Solution.
    Use (10.1.4) to calculate the RMS value of the voltage and current.
    🔗
    \begin{align*} |\vec{V_{RMS}}| \amp= \frac{120~\textrm{V}}{\sqrt{2}}\\ \amp= 84.9~\textrm{VRMS}\\ |\vec{I_{RMS}}| \amp= \frac{5~\textrm{A}}{\sqrt{2}}\\ \amp= 3.54~\textrm{ARMS} \end{align*}
    🔗
    Use (10.1.1) to calculate the phase difference.
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \theta \amp= 120^\circ - 60^\circ\\ \amp= 60^\circ \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗
    🔗

    Complex Power

    6.

    Calculate the power consumed by the load (indicated with dashed lines), as well as the power factor, for the circuit given in Figure 10.5.3. The source frequency is 50 Hz.
    🔗
    Answer.
    \begin{align*} \vec{S_{LOAD}} \amp= 3450 - j2560~\textrm{VA}\\ pf \amp= 0.804 \end{align*}
    🔗
    🔗
    Solution.
    Phasor transform the circuit.
    🔗
    Calculate the equivalent impedance of the load.
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \vec{Z_{LOAD}} \amp= ((10~\Omega) + (-j397.89~\Omega))//((90~\Omega) + (j48.69~\Omega) + (-j90.95~\Omega))\\ \amp= 70.47-j52.16~\Omega \end{align*}
    🔗
    Use (10.2.5) to calculate the power consumed by the load.
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \vec{S_{LOAD}} \amp= (7~\textrm{ARMS})^2(70.47-j52.16~\Omega)\\ \amp= 3450 - j2560~\textrm{VA} \end{align*}
    🔗
    Use (10.2.10) to calculate the power factor.
    🔗
    \begin{align*} pf \amp= \frac{3453}{\sqrt{3453^2 + 2559^2}}\\ \amp= 0.804 \end{align*}

    Maximum Power Transfer

    11.

    Determine the circuit elements that must be placed on the load (shown as \(\vec{Z_{LOAD}}\) in the circuit diagram) for maximum power transfer given the circuit in Figure 10.5.13. Then, calculate the power consumed by that load. The source frequency is 60 Hz.
    🔗
    Answer.
    The load circuit elements include a 205 Ω resistor connected in series with a 48.8 µF capacitor.
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} P = 76.2~\textrm{W} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
    Solution.
    Phasor transform the circuit.
    🔗
    Deactivate the source and calculate the Thévenin equivalent impedance.
    🔗
    \begin{align*} \vec{Z_{TH}} \amp= 190~\Omega + (100-j106.10~\Omega)//(j45.24~\Omega)\\ \amp= 204.93 + j54.33~\Omega \end{align*}
    🔗
    For maximum power transfer, the load must be equal to \(\vec{Z_{TH}}^*\text{.}\)
    🔗
