Exercise 7.

Calculate the power consumed by the load (indicated with dashed lines), as well as the power factor, for the circuit given in Figure 10.5.5. The source frequency is 60 Hz.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} \vec{S_{LOAD}} \amp= 339 + j132~\textrm{VA}\\ pf \amp= 0.932 \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Calculate the equivalent impedance of the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{LOAD}} \amp= ((50~\Omega)+(j40~\Omega))//(-j200~\Omega)\\ \amp= 71.17+j27.76~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use Ohm’s law to calculate the current through the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{I_{LOAD}} \amp= \frac{250~\textrm{VRMS}}{111.17+j27.76~\Omega}\\ \amp= 2.12-j0.53~\textrm{ARMS}\\ \amp= 2.18~\textrm{ARMS}\angle -14.02^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.2.5) to calculate the power consumed by the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{S_{LOAD}} \amp= (2.18~\textrm{ARMS})^2(71.17+j27.76~\Omega)\\ \amp= 339 + j132~\textrm{VA} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.2.10) to calculate the power factor.
🔗
\begin{align*} pf \amp= \frac{338.79}{\sqrt{338.79^2 + 132.13^2}}\\ \amp= 0.932 \end{align*}
