Exercise 9.

Calculate the power consumed by the load (indicated with dashed lines), as well as the power factor, for the circuit given in Figure 10.5.7. The source frequency is 60 Hz.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} \vec{S_{LOAD}} \amp= 557 - j203~\textrm{VA}\\ pf \amp= 0.940 \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Phasor transform the circuit.
🔗
Calculate the impedance of the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{LOAD}} \amp= (10~\Omega)//((2~\Omega)+(-j26.53~\Omega))\\ \amp= 8.58+j3.13~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Transform the voltage source to a current source.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{60+j103.92~\textrm{VRMS}}{5~\Omega} = 12+j20.78~\textrm{ARMS} \end{equation*}
🔗
Combine the 5 Ω and j7.54 Ω impedances in parallel.
🔗
\begin{equation*} (5~\Omega)//(j7.54~\Omega) = 3.47+j2.30~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Use the current divider rule to calculate the current through the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{I_{LOAD}} \amp= (12+j20.78~\textrm{ARMS})\left(\frac{(3.47+j2.30~\Omega)//(8.58+j6.90~\Omega)}{8.58+j6.90~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= 1.42+j7.93~\textrm{ARMS}\\ \amp= 8.06~\textrm{ARMS}\angle 79.83^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.2.5) to calculate the power consumed by the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{S_{LOAD}} \amp= (8.06~\textrm{ARMS})^2(8.58+j3.13~\Omega)\\ \amp= 557.38 - j203.20~\textrm{VA} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.2.10) to calculate the power factor.
🔗
\begin{align*} pf \amp= \frac{557.38}{\sqrt{557.38^2 + (-203.20)^2}}\\ \amp= 0.940 \end{align*}
