Exercise 10.

Calculate the power consumed by the load (indicated with dashed lines), as well as the power factor, for the circuit given in Figure 10.5.10. The source frequency is 50 Hz.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} \vec{S_{LOAD}} \amp= 631 + j1560~\textrm{VA}\\ pf \amp= 0.375 \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Phasor transform the circuit.
🔗
Calculate the impedance of the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{LOAD}} \amp= (j43.98~\Omega)//((65~\Omega)+(-j79.58~\Omega))\\ \amp= 22.89+j56.52~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use Ohm’s law to calculate the current through the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{I_{LOAD}} \amp= \frac{571.58+j330~\textrm{VRMS}}{(22.89+j56.52~\Omega)+(25+j59.69~\Omega)}\\ \amp= 4.16-j3.20~\textrm{ARMS}\\ \amp= 5.25~\textrm{ARMS}\angle -37.60^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.2.5) to calculate the power consumed by the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{S_{LOAD}} \amp= (5.25~\textrm{ARMS})^2(22.89+j56.52~\Omega)\\ \amp= 631.25 + j1558.36~\textrm{VA} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.2.10) to calculate the power factor.
🔗
\begin{align*} pf \amp= \frac{631.25}{\sqrt{631.25^2 + 1558.36^2}}\\ \amp= 0.375 \end{align*}
