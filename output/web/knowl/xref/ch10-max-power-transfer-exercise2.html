Exercise 12.

Determine the circuit elements that must be placed on the load (shown as \(\vec{Z_{LOAD}}\) in the circuit diagram) for maximum power transfer given the circuit in Figure 10.5.15. Then, calculate the power consumed by that load. The source frequency is 60 Hz.
🔗
Answer.
The load circuit elements include a 5.90 Ω resistor connected in series with a 263 µF capacitor.
🔗
\begin{equation*} P = 62.5~\textrm{W} \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Deactivate the source and calculate the Thévenin equivalent impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{TH}} \amp= j15~\Omega + (-j12~\Omega)//(10~\Omega)\\ \amp= 5.90+j10.08~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(\vec{V_{TH}}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_{TH}} \amp= (25+j43.30~\textrm{VRMS})\left(\frac{-j12~\Omega}{10-j12~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= 36.05+j13.26~\textrm{VRMS}\\ \amp= 38.41~\textrm{VRMS}\angle 20.19^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
For maximum power transfer, the load must be equal to \(\vec{Z_{TH}}^*\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} \vec{Z_{LOAD}} = 5.90-j10.08~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Use (8.2.4) to calculate the capacitance value.
🔗
\begin{align*} C \amp= -\frac{1}{2\pi (60)(-10.08)}\\ \amp= 263.10~\mu\textrm{F} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.3.2) to calculate the power transferred to the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} P_{LOAD,MAX} \amp= \frac{|38.41~\textrm{VRMS}|^2}{4(5.90~\Omega)}\\ \amp= 62.50~\textrm{W} \end{align*}
