Exercise 13.

Determine the circuit elements that must be placed on the load (shown as \(\vec{Z_{LOAD}}\) in the circuit diagram) for maximum power transfer given the circuit in Figure 10.5.16. Then, calculate the power consumed by that load. The source frequency is 50 Hz.
🔗
Answer.
The load circuit elements include a 79.8 Ω resistor connected in series with a 12.9 mH inductor.
🔗
\begin{equation*} P = 12.9~\textrm{mW} \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Phasor transform the circuit.
🔗
Deactivate the source and calculate the Thévenin equivalent impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{TH}} \amp= (-j1575.84~\Omega)//(80~\Omega)\\ \amp= 79.79-j4.05~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(\vec{V_{TH}}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_{TH}} \amp= (25.71+j30.64~\textrm{VRMS})\left(\frac{80~\Omega}{80-j1575.84~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= -1.49+j1.38~\textrm{VRMS}\\ \amp= 20.3~\textrm{VRMS}\angle 137.09^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
For maximum power transfer, the load must be equal to \(\vec{Z_{TH}}^*\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} \vec{Z_{LOAD}} = 79.79+j4.05~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Use (8.2.5) to calculate the inductance value.
🔗
\begin{align*} L \amp= \frac{4.05}{2\pi 50}\\ \amp= 12.89~\textrm{mH} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.3.2) to calculate the power transferred to the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} P_{LOAD,MAX} \amp= \frac{|20.3~\textrm{VRMS}|^2}{4(79.79~\Omega)}\\ \amp= 12.89~\textrm{mW} \end{align*}
