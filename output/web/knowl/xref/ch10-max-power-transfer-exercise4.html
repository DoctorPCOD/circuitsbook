Exercise 14.

Determine the circuit elements that must be placed on the load (shown as \(\vec{Z_{LOAD}}\) in the circuit diagram) for maximum power transfer given the circuit in Figure 10.5.18. Then, calculate the power consumed by that load. The source frequency is 50 Hz.
🔗
Answer.
The load circuit elements include a 11.2 Ω resistor connected in series with a 32.9 mH inductor.
🔗
\begin{equation*} P = 143~\textrm{W} \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Phasor transform the circuit.
🔗
Deactivate the source and calculate the Thévenin equivalent impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{TH}} \amp= 8~\Omega+(-j6.37~\Omega)//(5+j12.57~\Omega)\\ \amp= 11.19-j10.33~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Transform the current source into a voltage source.
🔗
\begin{equation*} (14.14+j14.14~\textrm{ARMS})(5~\Omega) = 70.71+j70.71~\textrm{VRMS} \end{equation*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(\vec{V_{TH}}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_{TH}} \amp= (70.71+j70.71~\textrm{VRMS})\left(\frac{-j6.37~\Omega}{5+j12.57-j6.37~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= -8.52-j79.47~\textrm{VRMS}\\ \amp= 79.93~\textrm{VRMS}\angle -96.12^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
For maximum power transfer, the load must be equal to \(\vec{Z_{TH}}^*\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} \vec{Z_{LOAD}} = 11.19+j10.33~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Use (8.2.5) to calculate the inductance value.
🔗
\begin{align*} L \amp= \frac{10.33}{2\pi 50}\\ \amp= 32.87~\textrm{mH} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.3.2) to calculate the power transferred to the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} P_{LOAD,MAX} \amp= \frac{|79.93~\textrm{VRMS}|^2}{4(11.19~\Omega)}\\ \amp= 142.67~\textrm{W} \end{align*}
