Exercise 15.

Determine the circuit elements that must be placed on the load (shown as \(\vec{Z_{LOAD}}\) in the circuit diagram) for maximum power transfer given the circuit in Figure 10.5.21. Then, calculate the power consumed by that load. The source frequency is 60 Hz.
🔗
Answer.
The load circuit elements include a 6.43 Ω resistor connected in series with a 657 µH inductor.
🔗
\begin{equation*} P = 553~\textrm{mW} \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Phasor transform the circuit.
🔗
Deactivate the source and calculate the Thévenin equivalent impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{TH}} \amp= (18~\Omega)//(10+j6.03-j6.63~\Omega)\\ \amp= 6.43-j0.25~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Convert the current source to a voltage source.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_S} \amp= (28~\textrm{ARMS})(-j6.63~\Omega)\\ \amp= -j185.68~\textrm{VRMS} \end{align*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(\vec{V_{TH}}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_{TH}} \amp= (-j185.68~\textrm{VRMS})\left(\frac{18~\Omega}{28+j6.03-j6.63~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= 2.55-j119.31~\textrm{VRMS}\\ \amp= 119.34~\textrm{VRMS}\angle -88.77^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
For maximum power transfer, the load must be equal to \(\vec{Z_{TH}}^*\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} \vec{Z_{LOAD}} = 6.43+j0.25~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Use (8.2.5) to calculate the inductance value.
🔗
\begin{align*} L \amp= \frac{0.25}{2\pi 50}\\ \amp= 656.99~\mu\textrm{H} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.3.2) to calculate the power transferred to the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} P_{LOAD,MAX} \amp= \frac{|119.34~\textrm{VRMS}|^2}{4(6.43~\Omega)}\\ \amp= 553.39~\textrm{W} \end{align*}
