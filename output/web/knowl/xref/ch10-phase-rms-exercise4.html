Exercise 4.

Calculate the RMS values of the magnitude of the source voltage and \(i(t)\text{,}\) as well as the phase difference between the two signals, given the circuit shown in Figure 10.5.1.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} |\vec{V_{RMS}}| \amp= 42.4~\textrm{VRMS}\\ |\vec{I_{RMS}}| \amp= 4.50~\textrm{ARMS}\\ \theta \amp= -32^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use (10.1.4) to calculate the RMS value of the voltage.
🔗
\begin{align*} |\vec{V_{RMS}}| \amp= \frac{60~\textrm{V}}{\sqrt{2}}\\ \amp= 42.4~\textrm{VRMS} \end{align*}
🔗
Use Ohm’s law to calculate \(\vec{I}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{I} \amp= \frac{39.87+14.51~\textrm{VRMS}}{8-j5~\Omega}\\ \amp= 2.77+j3.54~\textrm{ARMS}\\ \amp= 4.50~\textrm{ARMS} \angle 52^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.1.1) to calculate the phase difference.
🔗
\begin{align*} \theta \amp= 20^\circ - 52^\circ\\ \amp= -32^\circ \end{align*}
