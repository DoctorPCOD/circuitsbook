Exercise 5.

Calculate the RMS values of the magnitude of the source voltage and \(i(t)\text{,}\) as well as the phase difference between the two signals, given the circuit shown in Figure 10.5.2.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} |\vec{V_{RMS}}| \amp= 70.7~\textrm{VRMS}\\ |\vec{I_{RMS}}| \amp= 7.07~\textrm{ARMS}\\ \theta \amp= 53.1^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use (10.1.4) to calculate the RMS value of the voltage.
🔗
\begin{align*} |\vec{V_{RMS}}| \amp= \frac{100~\textrm{V}}{\sqrt{2}}\\ \amp= 70.7~\textrm{VRMS} \end{align*}
🔗
Use Ohm’s law to calculate \(\vec{I}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{I} \amp= \frac{70.71~\textrm{VRMS}}{6+j8~\Omega}\\ \amp= 4.24-j5.66~\textrm{ARMS}\\ \amp= 7.07~\textrm{ARMS} \angle -53.1^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.1.1) to calculate the phase difference.
🔗
\begin{align*} \theta \amp= 0^\circ - (-53.1^\circ)\\ \amp= 53.1^\circ \end{align*}
