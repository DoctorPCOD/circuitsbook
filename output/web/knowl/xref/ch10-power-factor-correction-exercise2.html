Exercise 17.

Calculate the initial power factor consumed by the load (indicated with dashed lines) in the circuit shown in Figure 10.5.26. Then, determine the circuit element that must be placed in parallel with the load to increase the power factor to 0.860. The source frequency is 60 Hz.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} pf_i \amp= 0.221\\ L \amp= 85.4~\textrm{mH} \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Phasor transform the circuit.
🔗
Calculate the load impedance.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \vec{Z_{LOAD}} = 6 - j26.53~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Use Ohm’s law to calculate the current through the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{I_{LOAD}} \amp= \frac{160~\textrm{VRMS}}{10-j26.53~\Omega}\\ \amp= 1.99+j5.28~\textrm{ARMS}\\ \amp= 5.64~\textrm{ARMS} \angle 69.34^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate the voltage dropped over the load.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_{LOAD}} \amp= (1.99+j5.28~\textrm{ARMS})(6-j26.53~\Omega)\\ \amp= 152.04-j21.13~\textrm{VRMS}\\ \amp= 153.50~\textrm{VRMS} \angle -7.91^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.2.5) to calculate the power consumed by the load. (You could also use (10.2.3) or (10.2.4) to calculate this value. Feel free to try the other two equations for extra practice.)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{S_{LOAD}} \amp= (5.64~\textrm{ARMS})^2(6 - j26.53~\Omega)\\ \amp= 191.14-j845.00~\textrm{VA} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.2.10) to calculate the initial power factor.
🔗
\begin{align*} pf \amp= \frac{191.14}{\sqrt{191.14^2 + (-845.00)^2}}\\ \amp= 0.221 \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.4.3) to calculate the final reactive power required to increase the power factor to the desired level.
🔗
\begin{align*} Q_f \amp= -191.14 \sqrt{\frac{1}{0.860^2}-1}\\ \amp= -113.41~\textrm{VAR} \end{align*}
🔗
Calculate \(Q_L\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} Q_L \amp= Q_f - Q\\ \amp= -113.41~\textrm{VAR} + 845.00~\textrm{VAR}\\ \amp= 731.59~\textrm{VAR} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (10.4.4) to calculate the compensating inductance.
🔗
\begin{align*} L \amp= \frac{|153.50|^2}{2\pi(60)(731.59)}\\ \amp= 85.43~\textrm{mH} \end{align*}
