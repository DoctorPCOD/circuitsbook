Derivation

\begin{align*} F(s) \amp= \frac{X+jY}{s+\alpha+j\beta} + \frac{X-jY}{s+\alpha-j\beta}\\ f(t) \amp= \left[(X+jY)~\textrm{e}^{(-\alpha-j\beta)t} + (X-jY)~\textrm{e}^{(-\alpha+j\beta)t} \right]~u(t)\\ \amp= \textrm{e}^{-\alpha t}\left[(X+jY)~\textrm{e}^{-j\beta t} + (X-jY)~\textrm{e}^{j\beta t}\right]~u(t)\\ \amp= \textrm{e}^{-\alpha t}\left[X~\textrm{e}^{-j\beta t} + X~\textrm{e}^{j\beta t} + jY~\textrm{e}^{-j\beta t} -jY~\textrm{e}^{j\beta t}\right]~u(t)\\ \amp= \textrm{e}^{-\alpha t}\left[2X\frac{\textrm{e}^{-j\beta t} + \textrm{e}^{j\beta t}}{2} + 2Y\frac{-\textrm{e}^{-j\beta t} +~\textrm{e}^{j\beta t}}{j2}\right]~u(t)\\ \amp= \textrm{e}^{-\alpha t}\left[2X~\cos(\beta t) + 2Y~\sin(\beta t)\right]~u(t) \end{align*}
