Exercise 12.

Use \(s\)-domain analysis to calculate \(V(s)\) of the circuit shown in Figure 11.4.4. The switch is open for a very long time before closing at a time of zero seconds.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} V(s) = \frac{\left[\frac{V_{DC}R_2}{R_1+R_2}\right]s}{s^2+\left[\frac{L+CR_1R_2}{LC(R_1+R_2)}\right]s + \left[\frac{R_1}{LC(R_1+R_2)}\right]} \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The initial current through the inductor and initial voltage drop over the capacitor are both zero, as no source is connected before the switch closes. Transform the circuit into the \(s\)-domain.
🔗
Perform KCL and KVL to obtain an equation in terms of \(V(s)\text{.}\) Then, solve for \(V(s)\) and normalize.
🔗
\begin{align*} \frac{\frac{V_{DC}}{s} - V_X}{R_1} \amp= \frac{V_X}{sL} + \frac{V(s)}{R_2}\\ \frac{V_{DC}}{R_1s} \amp= V_X\left[\frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{sL}\right] + \frac{V(s)}{R_2}\\ V_X \amp= \frac{V(s)}{sCR_2} + V(s)\\ \frac{V_{DC}}{R_1s} \amp= \left[\frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{sL}\right]\left[\frac{V(s)}{sCR_2} + V(s)\right]+ \frac{V(s)}{R_2}\\ \amp= \frac{V(s)}{sCR_1R_2} + \frac{V(s)}{R_1} + \frac{V(s)}{s^2LCR_2} + \frac{V(s)}{sL} + \frac{V(s)}{R_2}\\ \frac{sV_{DC}}{R_1} \amp= \left[\frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2}\right]s^2V(s) + \left[\frac{1}{CR_1R_2} + \frac{1}{L}\right]sV(s) + \left[\frac{1}{LCR_2}\right]V(s)\\ \frac{sR_2V_{DC}}{R_1+R_2} \amp= s^2V(s) + \left[\frac{L+CR_1R_2}{R_1+R_2}\right]sV(s) + \left[\frac{R_1}{LC(R_1+R_2)}\right]V(s)\\ V(s) \amp= \frac{\left[\frac{V_{DC}R_2}{R_1+R_2}\right]s}{s^2+\left[\frac{L+CR_1R_2}{LC(R_1+R_2)}\right]s + \left[\frac{R_1}{LC(R_1+R_2)}\right]} \end{align*}
