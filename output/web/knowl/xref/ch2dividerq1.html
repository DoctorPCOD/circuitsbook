Exercise 11.

Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(V_X\) in the circuit shown in Figure 2.10.31.
🔗
Answer.
4.8 V
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use the voltage divider rule.
🔗
\begin{align*} V_X \amp= 7.2~\textrm{V}\left(\frac{6~\textrm{k}\Omega}{3~\textrm{k}\Omega+6~\textrm{k}\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= 7.2~\textrm{V}(0.67)\\ \amp= 4.8~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
