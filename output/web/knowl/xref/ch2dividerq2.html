Exercise 12.

Use the current divider rule to calculate \(I_X\) in the circuit shown in Figure 2.10.32.
🔗
Answer.
140 mA
🔗
🔗
Solution.
First calculate the equivalent resistance of the circuit. All three resistors are connected in parallel.
🔗
\begin{align*} \frac{1}{R_{EQ}} \amp= \frac{1}{42~\Omega} + \frac{1}{12~\Omega} + \frac{1}{24~\Omega}\\ \amp= 0.0238~\Omega^{-1} + 0.083~\Omega^{-1} + 0.042~\Omega^{-1}\\ \amp= 0.149~\Omega^{-1}\\ R_{EQ} \amp= 6.72~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Now use the current divider rule to calculate \(I_X\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} I_X \amp= 500~\textrm{mA}\left(\frac{6.72~\Omega}{24~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= 140~\textrm{mA} \end{align*}
