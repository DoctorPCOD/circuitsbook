Exercise 13.

Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(V_X\) in the circuit shown in Figure 2.10.33.
🔗
Hint.
Read through Example 2.5.5 and check for similarities between the two circuits.
🔗
🔗
Answer.
3.21 V
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The voltage divider rule cannot be applied directly to this circuit yet as not all resistances are in series with the voltage source. An equivalent circuit to this was solved in Example 2.5.5. Use that analysis method to calculate \(V_X\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} V_X \amp= 8~\textrm{V}\left(\frac{470~\Omega}{100~\Omega+470~\Omega}\right)\left(\frac{330~\Omega//(100~\Omega+470~\Omega)}{220~\Omega+(330~\Omega//(100~\Omega+470~\Omega))}\right)\\ \amp= 8~\textrm{V}(0.8246)\left(\frac{330~\Omega//(570~\Omega)}{220~\Omega+(330~\Omega//570~\Omega)}\right)\\ \amp= 8~\textrm{V}(0.8246)\left(\frac{209~\Omega}{220~\Omega+209~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= 8~\textrm{V}(0.8246)(0.4872)\\ \amp= 3.21~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
