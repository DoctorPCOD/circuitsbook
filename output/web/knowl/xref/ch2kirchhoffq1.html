Exercise 16.

Calculate \(V_X\) in the circuit shown in Figure 2.10.38.
🔗
Answer.
3 V
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Apply KVL around the loop. This calculation will start in the lower left corner of the circuit and move around clockwise.
🔗
\begin{align*} 0 \amp= -5~\textrm{V} + 2~\textrm{V} + V_X\\ V_X \amp= 5~\textrm{V} - 2~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 3~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
