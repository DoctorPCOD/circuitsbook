Exercise 17.

Calculate \(I_X\) in the circuit shown in Figure 2.10.39.
🔗
Answer.
6 mA
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Apply KCL at one of the nodes. The top node will be used in this calculation.
🔗
\begin{align*} 0 \amp= 10~\textrm{mA} - 4~\textrm{mA} - I_X\\ I_X \amp= 10~\textrm{mA} - 4~\textrm{mA}\\ \amp= 6~\textrm{mA} \end{align*}
