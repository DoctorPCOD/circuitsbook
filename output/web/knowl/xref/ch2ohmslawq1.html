Exercise 6.

If the voltage source can supply a maximum current of 2 A, what is the minimum value of \(R_X\) that can be used in the circuit shown in Figure 2.10.26. (Assume the power rating of the resistor will be sufficient to handle this load.)
🔗
Answer.
3.7 Ω
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use Ohm’s law to calculate the equivalent resistance required to obtain a current of 2 A.
🔗
\begin{align*} R_{EQ} \amp= \frac{V}{I}\\ \amp = \frac{10~\textrm{V}}{2~\textrm{A}}\\ \amp = 5~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Because the two resistors are in series, use the equivalent resistance relationship to calculate the minimum value of \(R_X\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} R_{EQ} \amp= 1.3~\Omega + R_X\\ R_X \amp= R_{EQ} - 1.3~\Omega\\ \amp= 5~\Omega - 1.3~\Omega\\ \amp= 3.7~\Omega \end{align*}
