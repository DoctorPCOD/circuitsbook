Exercise 11.

Calculate the current through and power consumed by the 2 kΩ load resistor in the circuit shown in Figure 4.9.14. (Express this current as a positive value if it flows from \(V_{OUT}\) to ground, and negative if it flows from ground to \(V_{OUT}\text{.}\)) Then, calculate the current sourced by the op-amp. (Express this current as a positive value if the op-amp sources the current, and as a negative value if the op-amp sinks the current.)
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} I_{LOAD} \amp= 4~\textrm{mA}\\ P_{LOAD} \amp= 32~\textrm{mW}\\ I_{SOURCE} \amp= 4~\textrm{mA} \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The op-amp circuit is a voltage follower. The output voltage is equal to \(V_P\text{,}\) which is 8 V. Use Ohm’s law to calculate the current through the load resistor.
🔗
\begin{align*} I_{LOAD} \amp= \frac{8~\textrm{V}}{2~\textrm{k}\Omega}\\ \amp= 4~\textrm{mA} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (2.4.5) to calculate the power consumed by the resistor.
🔗
\begin{align*} P_{LOAD} \amp= (4~\textrm{mA})^2(2~\textrm{k}\Omega)\\ \amp= (16~\textrm{mA}^2)(2~\textrm{k}\Omega)\\ \amp= 32~\textrm{mW} \end{align*}
🔗
No current enters through the inverting node of the op-amp. Therefore, all of the current that flows through the 2 kΩ load resistor is sourced from the op-amp.
