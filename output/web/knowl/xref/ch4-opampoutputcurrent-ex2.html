Exercise 12.

Calculate the current through and power consumed by the 50 Ω load resistor in the circuit shown in Figure 4.9.15. (Express this current as a positive value if it flows from \(V_{OUT}\) to ground, and negative if it flows from ground to \(V_{OUT}\text{.}\)) Then, calculate the current sourced by the op-amp. (Express this current as a positive value if the op-amp sources the current, and as a negative value if the op-amp sinks the current.)
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} I_{LOAD} \amp= -40~\textrm{mA}\\ P_{LOAD} \amp= 80~\textrm{mW}\\ I_{SOURCE} \amp= -140~\textrm{mA} \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The op-amp circuit is an inverting op-amp. Apply (4.6.2) and (4.6.1) to calculate \(V_{OUT}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} V_{OUT} \amp= -(1~\textrm{V})\frac{20~\Omega}{10~\Omega}\\ \amp= -2~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Use Ohm’s law to calculate the current through the 50 Ω load resistor.
🔗
\begin{align*} I_{LOAD} \amp= \frac{-2~\textrm{V}}{0.05~\textrm{k}\Omega}\\ \amp= -40~\textrm{mA} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (2.4.5) to calculate the power consumed by the resistor.
🔗
\begin{align*} P_{LOAD} \amp= (-40~\textrm{mA})^2(0.05~\textrm{k}\Omega)\\ \amp= (1600~\textrm{mA}^2)(0.05~\textrm{k}\Omega)\\ \amp= 80~\textrm{mW} \end{align*}
🔗
The circuit diagram below is drawn with current directions.
🔗
Use KCL at the op-amp output node to calculate \(I_{SOURCE}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} I_{SOURCE} \amp= I_X - 40~\textrm{mA}\\ \amp= \frac{-2~\textrm{V} - 0~\textrm{V}}{0.02~\textrm{k}\Omega} - 40~\textrm{mA}\\ \amp= -100~\textrm{mA} - 40~\textrm{mA}\\ \amp= -140~\textrm{mA} \end{align*}
