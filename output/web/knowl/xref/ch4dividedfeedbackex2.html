Exercise 2.

Calculate the output voltage and circuit gain of the circuit shown in Figure 4.9.2.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} V_{OUT} \amp= 8~\textrm{V}\\ G \amp= 1.6 \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The circuit is a non-inverting op-amp circuit. Apply (4.6.3) to calculate the gain.
🔗
\begin{align*} G \amp= 1 + \frac{30~\textrm{k}\Omega}{50~\textrm{k}\Omega}\\ \amp= 1.6 \end{align*}
🔗
The voltage at the non-inverting node must be calculated before using (4.6.1) to calculate the output voltage. The voltage at the non-inverting node is equal to the voltage dropped over the 10 Ω resistor, which can be calculated with Ohm’s law. (To obtain a unit of V, first convert the source current to A to multiply with Ω. Alternatively, convert the resistance to kΩ to multiply with mA.)
🔗
\begin{align*} V_P \amp= (10~\Omega)(0.5~\textrm{A})\\ \amp= 5~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Now use (4.6.1) to calculate the output voltage.
🔗
\begin{align*} V_{OUT} \amp= (1.6)(5~\textrm{V})\\ \amp= 8~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
