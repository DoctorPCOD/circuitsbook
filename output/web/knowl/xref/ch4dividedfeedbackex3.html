Exercise 3.

Calculate the output voltage and source gains of the circuit shown in Figure 4.9.3. The circuit parameters are \(V_1 = 10~\textrm{mV}\) and \(V_2 = 300~\textrm{mV}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} V_{OUT} \amp= -0.65~\textrm{V}\\ G_1 \amp= -5\\ G_2 \amp= -2 \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The circuit is an inverting summing amplifier circuit. Apply (4.6.5) to calculate the output voltage and each of the source gains. The unit of each source has been converted to V.
🔗
\begin{align*} V_{OUT} \amp= -\left(\frac{10~\textrm{k}\Omega}{2~\textrm{k}\Omega}\right)V_1 -\left(\frac{10~\textrm{k}\Omega}{5~\textrm{k}\Omega}\right)V_2\\ \amp= -(5)(0.01~\textrm{V})-(2)(0.3~\textrm{V})\\ \amp= -0.05~\textrm{V} - 0.6~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= -0.65~\textrm{V}\\ G_1 \amp= -\frac{10~\textrm{k}\Omega}{2~\textrm{k}\Omega}\\ \amp= -5\\ G_2 \amp= -\frac{10~\textrm{k}\Omega}{5~\textrm{k}\Omega}\\ \amp= -2 \end{align*}
