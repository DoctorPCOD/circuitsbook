Exercise 5.

Calculate the output voltage and source gains of the circuit shown in Figure 4.9.5. The circuit parameters are \(V_1 = 3~\textrm{V}\) and \(V_2 = 5~\textrm{V}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} V_{OUT} \amp= 4~\textrm{V}\\ G_1 \amp= -2\\ G_2 \amp= 2 \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The circuit is a difference amplifier. To calculate the output voltage, apply (4.6.9).
🔗
\begin{align*} V_{OUT} \amp= -\left(\frac{2~\textrm{k}\Omega}{1~\textrm{k}\Omega}\right)3~\textrm{V} + \left(\frac{2~\textrm{k}\Omega+1~\textrm{k}\Omega}{1~\textrm{k}\Omega}\right)\left(\frac{10~\textrm{k}\Omega}{5~\textrm{k}\Omega+10~\textrm{k}\Omega}\right)5~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 4~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Apply (4.6.10) to calculate \(G_1\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} G_1 \amp= -\frac{2~\textrm{k}\Omega}{1~\textrm{k}\Omega}\\ \amp= -2 \end{align*}
🔗
Apply (4.6.11) to calculate \(G_2\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} G_2 \amp= \left(\frac{2~\textrm{k}\Omega+1~\textrm{k}\Omega}{1~\textrm{k}\Omega}\right)\left(\frac{10~\textrm{k}\Omega}{5~\textrm{k}\Omega+10~\textrm{k}\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= 2 \end{align*}
