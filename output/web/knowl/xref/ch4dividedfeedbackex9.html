Exercise 9.

Calculate the circuit gain of the circuit shown in Figure 4.9.10.
🔗
Answer.
\(G = 1.5\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
This circuit doesn’t fit any of the standard op-amp circuit templates (inverting, non-inverting, summing, or difference amplifier). Therefore, the assumptions of an op-amp circuit (no input current, virtual node) can be applied with any useful circuit laws and theorems to calculate these parameters.
🔗
Perform KCL at the non-inverting node of the left op-amp. The voltage at the non-inverting node will be referred to as \(V_P\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \frac{0-V_P}{3~\textrm{k}\Omega} \amp= \frac{V_P-V_{OUT}}{6~\textrm{k}\Omega}\\ -V_P \amp= 0.5V_P - 0.5V_{OUT}\\ -1.5V_P \amp= -0.5V_{OUT}\\ V_P \amp= \frac{1}{3}V_{OUT} \end{align*}
🔗
Perform KCL at the inverting node of the right op-amp. The output voltage of the left op-amp will be referred to as \(V_Y\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \frac{V_Y}{2~\textrm{k}\Omega} \amp= \frac{0-V_{OUT}}{6~\textrm{k}\Omega}\\ V_Y \amp= -\frac{1}{3}V_{OUT} \end{align*}
🔗
Perform KCL at the inverting node of the left op-amp. Then, plug in the values of \(V_P\) and \(V_Y\) defined above, and solve for \(V_{OUT}/V_S\text{,}\) which is equal to \(G\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \frac{V_S-V_P}{1~\textrm{k}\Omega} \amp= \frac{V_P-V_Y}{2~\textrm{k}\Omega}\\ V_S - V_P \amp= 0.5V_P - 0.5V_Y\\ V_S - \frac{1}{3}V_{OUT} \amp= 0.5(\frac{1}{3}V_{OUT}) - 0.5(-\frac{1}{3}V_{OUT})\\ V_S \amp= \frac{1}{3}V_{OUT} + \frac{1}{6}V_{OUT} + \frac{1}{6}V_{OUT}\\ V_S \amp= \frac{4}{6}V_{OUT}\\ \frac{V_{OUT}}{V_S} \amp= 1.5 \end{align*}
