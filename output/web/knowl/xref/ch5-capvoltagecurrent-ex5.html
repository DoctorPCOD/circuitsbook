Exercise 10.

Figure 5.8.24 depicts \(v(t)\) for a 2 µF capacitor. Calculate \(\tau\text{,}\) \(R_{EQ}\) as seen by the capacitor, and an equation for \(i(t)\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} \tau \amp= 1.67~\textrm{ms}\\ R_{EQ} \amp= 837~\Omega\\ i(t) \amp= 21.5~\textrm{mA}~\textrm{e}^{-597t}~u(t) \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Analyze the known coordinate (3 ms, 15 V) using (5.7.10) to calculate \(\tau\text{.}\) (Because a unit of ms is used for the variable \(t\text{,}\) the unit of \(\tau\) in the calculation will also be ms.)
🔗
\begin{align*} v(t) \amp= 15~\textrm{V} \amp= 18~\textrm{V}\left[1-\textrm{e}^{-\frac{3}{\tau}}\right]\\ \frac{15~\textrm{V}}{18~\textrm{V}} \amp= 1-\textrm{e}^{-\frac{3}{\tau}}\\ 0.83 \amp= 1-\textrm{e}^{-\frac{3}{\tau}}\\ -0.167 \amp= -\textrm{e}^{-\frac{3}{\tau}}\\ 0.167 \amp= \textrm{e}^{-\frac{3}{\tau}}\\ \ln{0.167} \amp= \ln{\textrm{e}^{-\frac{3}{\tau}}}\\ -1.792 \amp= -\frac{3}{\tau}\\ 1.792 \amp= \frac{3}{\tau}\\ \tau \amp= \frac{3}{1.792}\\ \amp= 1.67~\textrm{ms} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (5.7.5) to calculate the equivalent resistance as seen by the capacitor.
🔗
\begin{align*} RC \amp= 1.67\times10^{-3}~\textrm{s}\\ R \amp= \frac{1.67\times10^{-3}~\textrm{s}}{C}\\ \amp= \frac{1.67\times10^{-3}~\textrm{s}}{2\times10^{-6}~\textrm{F}}\\ \amp= 837~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use (5.7.11) to derive an equation for \(i(t)\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} i(t) \amp= \frac{18~\textrm{V}}{837~\Omega}\textrm{e}^{-597t}~u(t)\\ \amp= \frac{18~\textrm{V}}{0.837~\textrm{k}\Omega}\textrm{e}^{-597t}~u(t)\\ \amp= 21.5~\textrm{mA}~\textrm{e}^{-597t}~u(t) \end{align*}
