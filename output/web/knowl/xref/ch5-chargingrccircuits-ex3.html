Exercise 18.

Calculate an expression for \(v(t)\) given the circuit shown in Figure 5.8.48. The switch is in position a for a very long time before moving to position b at a time of zero seconds. The circuit parameters are: \(R_1 = 100~\Omega\text{,}\) \(R_2 = 400~\Omega\text{,}\) \(C = 20~\textrm{mF}\text{,}\) and \(V_S = 2~\textrm{V}\text{.}\) Assume that the op-amp is sufficiently sourced such that the output never saturates.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} v(t) = -8~\textrm{V}~\left[1-\textrm{e}^{-0.125t}\right]~u(t) \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
In the initial steady-state, the capacitor will be fully discharged and have an initial voltage of zero. Draw the circuit in the final steady-state. The capacitor can be replaced as an open.
🔗
This is an inverting op-amp circuit. Use (4.5.2) to calculate \(v(\infty)\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} v(\infty) \amp= -V_S\frac{R_2}{R_1}\\ \amp= -(2~\textrm{V})\frac{400~\Omega}{100~\Omega}\\ \amp= -8~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Analyze the circuit at \(t = 0^+\text{.}\)
🔗
Use KCL at the inverting input to obtain a differential equation in terms of \(v(t)\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} I_1 \amp = I_2 + I_3\\ \frac{V_S-0}{R_1} \amp= \frac{0-v(t)}{R_2} + C\frac{d}{dt}[0-v(t)]\\ \frac{V_S}{R_1} \amp= \frac{-v(t)}{R_2} + C\frac{d}{dt}[-v(t)]\\ -\frac{V_S}{R_1} \amp= \frac{v(t)}{R_2} + C\frac{dv(t)}{dt}\\ -\frac{V_S}{CR_1} \amp= \frac{dv(t)}{dt} + \frac{v(t)}{CR_2} \end{align*}
🔗
Analyze the coefficient \(a_0\) to calculate \(\tau\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \tau \amp= R_2C\\ \amp= (400~\Omega)(0.02~\textrm{F})\\ \amp= 8~\textrm{s} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (5.7.12) to derive an equation for \(v(t)\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} v(t) \amp= v(\infty)\left[1-\textrm{e}^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}\right]~u(t)\\ \amp= -8~\textrm{V}~\left[1-\textrm{e}^{-0.125t}\right]~u(t) \end{align*}
