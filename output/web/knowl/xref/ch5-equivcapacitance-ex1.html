Exercise 1.

Calculate the equivalent capacitance between nodes a and b of the circuit shown in Figure 5.8.1.
🔗
Answer.
67.2 pF
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Start by combining the 320 pF and 100 pF capacitors. They’re connected in parallel, so the two capacitances sum together to 420 pF.
🔗
The three remaining capacitors are in parallel. Use the reciprocal sum to calculate the equivalent resistance.
🔗
\begin{align*} C_{EQ} \amp= \frac{1}{\frac{1}{240~\textrm{pF}} + \frac{1}{120~\textrm{pF}} + \frac{1}{420~\textrm{pF}}}\\ \amp= \frac{1}{\frac{25}{1680~\textrm{pF}}}\\ \amp= \frac{1680~\textrm{pF}}{25}\\ \amp= 67.2~\textrm{pF} \end{align*}
