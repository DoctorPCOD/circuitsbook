Exercise 2.

Calculate the equivalent capacitance between nodes a and b of the circuit shown in Figure 5.8.3.
🔗
Answer.
6.38 nF
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Start by combining the capacitors farthest to the right in the diagram. These are the 10 nF and 33 nF capacitors, which are connected in series. The equivalent capacitance of these two capacitors is
🔗
\begin{align*} \amp= \frac{(10~\textrm{nF})(33~\textrm{nF})}{10~\textrm{nF} + 33~\textrm{nF}}\\ \amp= \frac{330~\textrm{nF}^2}{43~\textrm{nF}}\\ \amp= 7.674~\textrm{nF}. \end{align*}
🔗
Now, combine the 47 nF, 33 nF, and 7.674 nF capacitors, which are in parallel. The capacitances sum to 87.674 nF.
🔗
These three capacitors are in parallel. Use the reciprocal sum formula to calculate the total equivalent capacitance. (The 87.674 nF capacitance is represented as a fraction in the calculation below.)
🔗
\begin{align*} C_{EQ} \amp= \frac{1}{\frac{1}{10~\textrm{nF}} + \frac{1}{22~\textrm{nF}} + \frac{43}{3770~\textrm{nF}}}\\ \amp= \frac{1}{\frac{6505}{41470~\textrm{nF}}}\\ \amp= \frac{41470~\textrm{nF}}{6505}\\ \amp= 6.38~\textrm{nF} \end{align*}
