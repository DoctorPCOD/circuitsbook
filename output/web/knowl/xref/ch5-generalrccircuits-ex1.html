Exercise 21.

Derive equations for \(v(t)\) and \(i(t)\) given the circuit shown in Figure 5.8.62. The switch is open for a very long time before closing at a time of zero seconds.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} v(t) \amp= \left[ 16.875~\textrm{V} - 4.875~\textrm{V}~\textrm{e}^{-6.85t} \right]~u(t) + 12~\textrm{V}~u(-t)\\ i(t) \amp= 1.34~\textrm{mA}~\textrm{e}^{-6.85t}~u(t) \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Draw the circuit in the initial steady-state condition. The capacitor can be replaced by an open.
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(v(0)\text{.}\) (Note: the 1.4 kΩ resistor does not contribute to the circuit in this steady-state condition and can be ignored.)
🔗
\begin{align*} v(0) \amp= 24~\textrm{V}\left(\frac{6~\textrm{k}\Omega}{6~\textrm{k}\Omega+6~\textrm{k}\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= 12~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Draw the circuit in the final steady-state condition. The capacitor can be replaced by an open.
🔗
The 1.4 kΩ resistor does not contribute to the circuit in this steady-state condition and can be removed from the circuit diagram.
🔗
Combine the two parallel resistors to simplify the circuit.
🔗
Transform the 24 V source to a current source. This will cause all circuit elements to be in parallel, simplifying the analysis. The new current source will have a value of
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{24~\textrm{V}}{6~\textrm{k}\Omega} = 4~\textrm{mA}. \end{equation*}
🔗
Combine the two current sources and combine the two parallel resistors.
🔗
Use Ohm’s law to calculate \(v(\infty)\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} v(\infty) \amp= (7.5~\textrm{mA})(2.25~\textrm{k}\Omega)\\ \amp= 16.875~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Draw the circuit at \(t=0^+\) and deactivate the sources to calculate the equivalent resistance seen by the capacitor.
🔗
Calculate the equivalent resistance as seen by the capacitor.
🔗
\begin{align*} R_{EQ} \amp= 9~\textrm{k}\Omega//6~\textrm{k}\Omega//6~\textrm{k}\Omega+1.4~\textrm{k}\Omega\\ \amp= 9~\textrm{k}\Omega//3~\textrm{k}\Omega+1.4~\textrm{k}\Omega\\ \amp= 2.25~\textrm{k}\Omega+1.4~\textrm{k}\Omega\\ \amp= 3.65~\textrm{k}\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use (5.7.5) to calculate the RC time constant.
🔗
\begin{align*} \tau \amp= (3650~\Omega)(40\times10^{-6}~\textrm{F})\\ \amp= 0.146~\textrm{s} \end{align*}
🔗
Plug these values into (5.7.12) to calculate \(v(t)\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} v(t) = \left[ 16.875~\textrm{V} - 4.875~\textrm{V}~\textrm{e}^{-6.85t} \right]~u(t) + 12~\textrm{V}~u(-t) \end{equation*}
🔗
Use (5.7.13) to calculate \(i(t)\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} i(t) = 1.34~\textrm{mA}~\textrm{e}^{-6.85t}~u(t) \end{equation*}
