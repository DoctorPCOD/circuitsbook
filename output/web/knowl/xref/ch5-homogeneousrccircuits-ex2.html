Exercise 12.

Calculate an expression for \(v(t)\) and \(i(t)\) given the circuit shown in Figure 5.8.30. The switch is closed for a very long time before opening at a time of zero seconds.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} v(t) \amp= 8~\textrm{V}~\textrm{e}^{-5.88t}~u(t) + 8~\textrm{V}~u(-t)\\ i(t) \amp= -0.42~\textrm{mA}~\textrm{e}^{-5.88t}~u(t) \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Draw the circuit in the initial steady-state condition. The capacitor can be replaced as an open.
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(v(0)\text{.}\) (Refer to Example 2.5.5 for using the voltage divider rule in a two-mesh circuit.)
🔗
\begin{align*} v(0) \amp= 18~\textrm{V}\left(\frac{36~\textrm{k}\Omega//(15~\textrm{k}\Omega+30~\textrm{k}\Omega)}{10~\textrm{k}\Omega+36~\textrm{k}\Omega//(15~\textrm{k}\Omega+30~\textrm{k}\Omega)}\right)\left(\frac{30~\textrm{k}\Omega}{45~\textrm{k}\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= 18~\textrm{V}\left(\frac{36~\textrm{k}\Omega//45~\textrm{k}\Omega}{10~\textrm{k}\Omega+36~\textrm{k}\Omega//45~\textrm{k}\Omega}\right)\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)\\ \amp= 18~\textrm{V}\left(\frac{20~\textrm{k}\Omega}{10~\textrm{k}\Omega+20~\textrm{k}\Omega}\right)\left(\frac{2}{3}\right)\\ \amp= 8~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Analyze the circuit at \(t=0^+\) to calculate the equivalent resistance seen by the capacitor. The circuit is shown in this condition below.
🔗
Calculate the equivalent resistance as seen by the capacitor.
🔗
\begin{align*} R_{EQ} \amp= 30~\textrm{k}\Omega//(15~\textrm{k}\Omega+36~\textrm{k}\Omega)\\ \amp= 30~\textrm{k}\Omega//51~\textrm{k}\Omega\\ \amp= 18.89~\textrm{k}\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use (5.7.6) to calculate the RC time constant.
🔗
\begin{align*} \tau \amp= RC\\ \amp= (18888.89~\Omega)(9~\mu\textrm{F})\\ \amp= 0.17~\textrm{s} \end{align*}
🔗
Plug these values into (5.7.5) to derive an expression for \(v(t)\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} v(t) = 8~\textrm{V}~\textrm{e}^{-5.88t}~u(t) + 8~\textrm{V}~u(-t) \end{equation*}
🔗
Use (5.7.7) to calculate the current.
🔗
\begin{equation*} i(t) = -0.42~\textrm{mA}~\textrm{e}^{-5.88t}~u(t) \end{equation*}
