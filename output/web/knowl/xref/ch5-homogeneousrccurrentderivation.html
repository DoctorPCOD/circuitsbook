Derivation

\begin{align*} i(t) \amp= C\frac{dv(t)}{dt}\\ \amp= C\frac{d}{dt}\left(v(0^+) \textrm{e}^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}\right)\\ \amp= Cv(0^+)\frac{d}{dt}\left(\textrm{e}^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}\right)\\ \amp= -\frac{Cv(0^+)}{\tau}\textrm{e}^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}\\ \amp= -\frac{Cv(0^+)}{RC}\textrm{e}^{-\frac{t}{\tau}}\\ \amp= -\frac{v(0^+)}{R}\textrm{e}^{-\frac{t}{\tau}} \end{align*}
🔗
Because this was derived for times where \(t \geq 0\text{,}\) that can be explicitly described mathematically by multiplying the equation by the unit step function.
