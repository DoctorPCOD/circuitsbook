Derivation

\begin{align*} \frac{v_{in}(t)}{R} \amp= C\frac{d}{dt}\left[-v_{out}(t)\right]\\ \int \frac{v_{in}(t)}{R}\, dt \amp= \int C\frac{d}{dt}\left[-v_{out}(t)\right]\, dt\\ \frac{1}{R} \int v_{in}(t) \, dt \amp= -C v_{out}(t)\\ -\frac{1}{CR} \int v_{in}(t) \, dt \amp= v_{out}(t) \end{align*}
