Exercise 16.

Derive equations for \(i(t)\) and \(v(t)\) given the circuit shown in Figure 6.6.56. The switch is open for a very long time before closing at a time of zero seconds.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} i(t) \amp= 0.5~\textrm{mA}~\left[1 - \textrm{e}^{-80000t}\right]~u(t)\\ v(t) \amp= 4~\textrm{V}~\textrm{e}^{-80000t}~u(t) \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Analyze the circuit in the final steady-state condition. The inductor can be replaced by a short.
🔗
Use source transformation to convert the voltage source to a current source.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{8~\textrm{V}}{4~\textrm{k}\Omega} = 2~\textrm{mA} \end{equation*}
🔗
Calculate the equivalent resistance of the circuit.
🔗
\begin{align*} R_{EQ} \amp= \frac{1}{\frac{1}{4~\textrm{k}\Omega} + \frac{1}{5~\textrm{k}\Omega} + \frac{1}{6~\textrm{k}\Omega} + \frac{1}{20~\textrm{k}\Omega}}\\ \amp= 1.5~\textrm{k}\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use the current divider rule to calculate \(i(\infty)\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} i(\infty) \amp= 2~\textrm{mA}\left(\frac{1.5~\textrm{k}\Omega}{6~\textrm{k}\Omega}\right)\\ =\amp= 0.5~\textrm{mA} \end{align*}
🔗
Draw the circuit at \(t=0^+\) and deactivate the source to calculate the equivalent resistance as seen by the inductor.
🔗
Calculate the equivalent resistance as seen by the inductor.
🔗
\begin{align*} R_{EQ} \amp= 4~\textrm{k}\Omega//5~\textrm{k}\Omega//20~\textrm{k}\Omega+6~\textrm{k}\Omega\\ \amp= 2~\textrm{k}\Omega+6~\textrm{k}\Omega\\ \amp= 8~\textrm{k}\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use (6.5.4) to calculate \(\tau\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \tau \amp= \frac{L}{R}\\ \amp= \frac{0.1~\textrm{H}}{8000~\Omega}\\ \amp= 1.25\times10^{-5}~\textrm{s} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (6.5.6) to calculate \(i(t)\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} i(t) = 0.5~\textrm{mA}~\left[1 - \textrm{e}^{-80000t}\right]~u(t) \end{equation*}
🔗
Use (6.5.7) to calculate \(v(t)\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} v(t) \amp= (8~\textrm{k}\Omega)(0.5~\textrm{mA}~\textrm{e}^{-80000t}~u(t))\\ \amp= 4~\textrm{V}~\textrm{e}^{-80000t}~u(t) \end{align*}
