Exercise 1.

Calculate the equivalent inductance of the circuit shown in Figure 6.6.1.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} L_{EQ} = 74.4~\textrm{mH} \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Start by combining the 20 mH, 50 mH, and 60 mH inductors in series.
🔗
Combine the 30 mH and 130 mH inductors in parallel.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{(30~\textrm{mH})(130~\textrm{mH})}{30~\textrm{mH}+130~\textrm{mH}} = 24.375~\textrm{mH} \end{equation*}
🔗
All the remaining inductors are in series. Add the values together to calculate the equivalent inductance.
🔗
\begin{align*} L_{EQ} \amp= 10~\textrm{mH} + 40~\textrm{mH} + 24.375~\textrm{mH}\\ \amp= 74.4~\textrm{mH} \end{align*}
