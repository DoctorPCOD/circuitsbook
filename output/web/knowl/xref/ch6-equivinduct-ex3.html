Exercise 3.

Calculate the value of inductor \(L_X\) given that the circuit shown in Figure 6.6.8 has an equivalent inductance of 250 µH.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} L_X = 300~\mu\textrm{H} \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use the equivalent inductance relationships to calculate \(L_X\text{.}\) The unit used for inductance in the equations below is µH.
🔗
\begin{align*} L_{EQ} \amp= 250\\ 250 \amp= (300+L_X)//200+100\\ 150 \amp= \frac{200(300+L_X)}{200+300+L_X}\\ \amp= \frac{60000 + 200L_X}{500+L_X}\\ 75000+150L_X \amp= 60000+200L_X\\ 15000 \amp= 50L_X\\ 300 \amp= L_X\\ L_X \amp= 300~\mu\textrm{H} \end{align*}
