Exercise 20.

Derive the differential equation in terms of \(v(t)\) for the circuit shown in Figure 7.8.70. Assume that the op-amp is sufficiently sourced such that the output will never saturate.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{align*} -\frac{1}{R_1C_2}\frac{dv_{in}}{dt} \amp= \frac{d^2v(t)}{dt^2} + \left(\frac{1}{C_1R_F}+\frac{1}{C_2R_F}\right)\frac{dv(t)}{dt}\\ \amp+ \left(\frac{1}{C_1C_2R_1R_F}+\frac{1}{C_1C_2R_2R_F}\right)v(t) \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Perform KCL at the node connecting \(R_1\text{,}\) \(R_2\text{,}\) \(C_1\text{,}\) and \(C_2\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{equation} \frac{v_{in}(t) - v_X(t)}{R_1} = \frac{v_X(t)}{R_2} + C_2 \frac{d}{dt}\left(v_X(t) - v(t)\right) + C_1\frac{dv_X(t)}{dt}\tag{7.8.49} \end{equation}
🔗
Perform KCL at the inverting input of the op-amp. Then, solve for \(v_X(t)\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align} C_1\frac{dv_X(t)}{dt} \amp= \frac{-v(t)}{R_F}\tag{7.8.50}\\ \frac{dv_X(t)}{dt} \amp= \frac{-v(t)}{C_1R_F}\tag{7.8.51}\\ v_X(t) \amp= -\frac{1}{C_1R_F}\int v(t) \,dt\tag{7.8.52} \end{align}
🔗
Plug (7.8.52) into (7.8.49) and reduce until the differential equation is in standard form.
🔗
\begin{align*} -\frac{v_{in}(t)}{R_1} \amp= \frac{1}{R_1C_1R_F}\int v(t) \,dt + \frac{1}{R_2C_1R_F}\int v(t) \,dt + \frac{C_2}{C_1R_F}v(t)\\ \amp+ C_2 \frac{dv(t)}{dt} + \frac{C_1}{C_1R_F}v(t)\\ -\frac{1}{R_1}\frac{dv_{in}}{dt} \amp= \frac{1}{C_1R_1R_F}v(t) + \frac{1}{C_1R_2R_F}v(t) + \frac{C_2}{C_1R_F}\frac{dv(t)}{dt}\\ \amp+ C_2\frac{d^2v(t)}{dt^2} + \frac{1}{R_F}\frac{dv(t)}{dt}\\ -\frac{1}{R_1C_2}\frac{dv_{in}}{dt} \amp= \frac{d^2v(t)}{dt^2} + \left(\frac{1}{C_1R_F}+\frac{1}{C_2R_F}\right)\frac{dv(t)}{dt}\\ \amp+ \left(\frac{1}{C_1C_2R_1R_F}+\frac{1}{C_1C_2R_2R_F}\right)v(t) \end{align*}
