Derivation

Start by solving (7.5.7) when \(t=0^+\text{.}\) This conveniently derives coefficient \(A_1\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} v(0^+) \amp= A_1\textrm{e}^{-\alpha *0} + A_2t\textrm{e}^{-\alpha *0}\\ v(0^+) \amp= A_1 \end{align*}
🔗
Calculate the first derivative of (7.5.7) and solve when \(t=0^+\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \frac{d}{dt}v(t) \amp= \frac{d}{dt}\left(A_1\textrm{e}^{-\alpha t} + A_2t\textrm{e}^{-\alpha t}\right)\\ v'(t) \amp= -\alpha A_1\textrm{e}^{-\alpha t} + \textrm{e}^{-\alpha t}(A_2 - A_2\alpha t)\\ v'(0^+) \amp= -\alpha A_1\textrm{e}^{-\alpha *0} + \textrm{e}^{-\alpha *0}(A_2 - A_2\alpha *0)\\ v'(0^+) \amp= -\alpha A_1 + A_2 \end{align*}
🔗
Use the equations for \(v(0^+)\) and \(v'(0^+)\) to solve for \(A_2\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} v'(0^+) \amp= -\alpha A_1 + A_2\\ v'(0^+) + \alpha A_1 \amp= A_2\\ A_2 \amp= v'(0^+) + \alpha v(0^+) \end{align*}
