Derivation

\begin{align*} 0 \amp= \frac{v(t)}{R} + i(t) + C\frac{dv(t)}{dt}\\ \amp= \frac{1}{R}\left(L\frac{di(t)}{dt}\right) + i(t) + C\frac{d}{dt}\left(L\frac{di(t)}{dt}\right)\\ \amp= LC\frac{d^2i(t)}{dt^2} + \frac{L}{R}\frac{di(t)}{dt} + i(t)\\ \amp= \frac{d^2i(t)}{dt^2} + \frac{1}{RC}\frac{di(t)}{dt} + \frac{1}{LC}i(t) \end{align*}
