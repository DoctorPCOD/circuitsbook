Derivation

Start by solving (7.5.4) when \(t=0^+\text{.}\) This conveniently derives coefficient \(B_1\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} v(0^+) \amp= \textrm{e}^{-\alpha*0} \left[ B_1\cos{(\beta *0)} + B_2\sin{(\beta *0)} \right]\\ v(0^+) \amp= B_1 \end{align*}
🔗
Calculate the first derivative of (7.5.4) and solve when \(t=0^+\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \frac{d}{dt}v(t) \amp= \frac{d}{dt}\left(\textrm{e}^{-\alpha t} \left[ B_1\cos{\beta t} + B_2\sin{\beta t} \right]\right)\\ v'(t) \amp= \textrm{e}^{-\alpha t}\left[(\cos{\beta t})(B_2\beta - \alpha B_1) - (\sin{\beta t})(\alpha B_2 + \beta B_1)\right]\\ v'(0^+) \amp= \textrm{e}^{-\alpha *0}\left[(\cos{(\beta *0)})(B_2\beta - \alpha B_1) - (\sin{(\beta *0)})(\alpha B_2 + \beta B_1)\right]\\ v'(0^+) \amp= B_2\beta - \alpha B_1 \end{align*}
🔗
Use the equations for \(v(0^+)\) and \(v'(0^+)\) to solve for \(B_2\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} v'(0^+) \amp= B_2\beta - \alpha B_1\\ v'(0^+) + \alpha B_1 \amp= B_2\beta\\ B_2 \amp= \frac{v'(0^+) + \alpha B_1}{\beta}\\ B_2 \amp= \frac{v'(0^+) + \alpha v(0^+)}{\beta} \end{align*}
