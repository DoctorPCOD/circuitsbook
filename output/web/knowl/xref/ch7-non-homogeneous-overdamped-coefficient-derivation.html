Calculate the first derivative of (7.6.3) and solve when \(t=0^+\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \frac{d}{dt}v(t) \amp= \frac{d}{dt}\left(v_P(0^+) + A_1\textrm{e}^{s_1t} + A_2\textrm{e}^{s_2t}\right)\\ v'(t) \amp= s_1A_1\textrm{e}^{s_1t} + s_2A_2\textrm{e}^{s_2t}\\ v'(0^+) \amp= s_1A_1\textrm{e}^{s_1*0} + s_2A_2\textrm{e}^{s_2*0}\\ v'(0^+) \amp= s_1A_1 + s_2A_2 \end{align*}

Use the equations for \(v(0^+)\) and \(v'(0^+)\) to solve for \(A_1\) and \(A_2\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} A_1 \amp= v(0) - v(\infty) - A_2\\ v'(0^+) \amp= (v(0) - v(\infty) - A_2)s_1 + A_2s_2\\ \amp= v(0^+)s_1 - v(\infty)s_1 - A_2s_1 + A_2s_2\\ A_2 (s_2-s_1) \amp= v'(0^+) - v(0^+)s_1 + v(\infty)s_2\\ A_2 \amp= \frac{v'(0^+) - v(0^+)s_1 + v(\infty)s_2}{s_2-s_1}\\ A_1 \amp= v(0) - v(\infty) - \frac{v'(0^+) - v(0^+)s_1 + v(\infty)s_2}{s_2-s_1}\\ A_1(s_2-s_1) \amp= v(0^+)(s_2-s_1) - v(\infty)(s_2-s_1) - v'(0^+)\\ \amp+ s_1v(0^+) - s_1v(\infty)\\ \amp= v(0^+)s_2 - v(0^+)s_1 + v(0^+)s_1 - v(\infty)s_2\\ \amp+ v(\infty)s_1 - v(\infty)s_1 - v'(0^+)\\ \amp= v(0^+)s_2 - v(\infty)s_2 - v'(0^+)\\ A_1 \amp= \frac{v(0^+)s_2 - v(\infty)s_2 - v'(0^+)}{s_2-s_1} \end{align*}
