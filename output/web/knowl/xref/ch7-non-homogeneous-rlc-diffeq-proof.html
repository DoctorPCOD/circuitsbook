Derivation

\begin{align*} V_{IN} \amp= Ri(t) + L\frac{di(t)}{dt} + v(t)\\ \amp= R\left(C \frac{dv(t)}{dt}\right) + L\frac{d}{dt}\left(C \frac{dv(t)}{dt}\right) + v(t)\\ \amp= LC \frac{d^2v(t)}{dt^2} + RC \frac{dv(t)}{dt} + v(t)\\ \frac{V_{IN}}{LC} \amp= \frac{d^2v(t)}{dt^2} + \frac{R}{L} \frac{dv(t)}{dt} + \frac{1}{LC}v(t) \end{align*}
