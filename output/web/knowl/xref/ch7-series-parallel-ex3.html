Exercise 3.

Derive an equation for \(v(t)\) for the circuit shown in Figure 7.8.9. The inductor has a parasitic resistance of 10 Ω, which is not explicitly shown in the circuit diagram. The switch is in position a for a very long time before moving to position b at a time of zero seconds.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} v(t) = \left(\textrm{e}^{-27.8t}\left[28~\textrm{V}~\cos{127t} + 6.14~\textrm{V}~\sin{127t}\right]\right)~u(t) + 28~\textrm{V}~u(-t) \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Calculate the initial conditions of the circuit. The capacitor can be replaced by an open and the inductor can be replaced by its parasitic resistance.
🔗
The initial voltage is equal to 28 V. There is no current through the inductor in this initial steady-state configuration.
🔗
Analyze the circuit at \(t=0^+\text{.}\)
🔗
This is a homogeneous series RLC circuit.
🔗
By looking at (7.3.5), we can see that the initial condition for the first derivative of voltage will be equal to zero.
🔗
Use (7.3.3) to calculate the damping parameter. Be sure to include the parasitic resistance of the inductor in the resistance value in this calculation!
🔗
\begin{align*} \alpha \amp= \frac{10~\Omega}{2(0.18~\textrm{H})}\\ \amp= 27.7778~\textrm{Np/s} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (7.3.4) to calculate the resonant frequency.
🔗
\begin{align*} \omega_0 \amp= \frac{1}{\sqrt{(0.18~\textrm{H})(330 \times 10^{-6}~\textrm{F})}}\\ \amp= 129.7498~\textrm{rad/s} \end{align*}
🔗
The circuit is underdamped. Use (7.2.5) to calculate the oscillation frequency.
🔗
\begin{align*} \beta \amp= \sqrt{(129.7498~\textrm{rad/s})^2 - (27.7778~\textrm{Np/s})^2}\\ \amp= 126.7415~\textrm{rad/s} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (7.5.5) and (7.5.6) to calculate the coefficients.
🔗
\begin{align*} B_1 \amp= 28~\textrm{V}\\ B_2 \amp= \frac{0~\textrm{V/s} + (27.7778~\textrm{Np/s})(28~\textrm{V})}{126.7415~\textrm{rad/s}}\\ \amp= 6.1367~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Plug these values into (7.5.4) to derive an equation for \(v(t)\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{equation*} v(t) = \left(\textrm{e}^{-27.8t}\left[26~\textrm{V}~\cos{127t} + 6.14~\textrm{V}~\sin{127t}\right]\right)~u(t) + 26~\textrm{V}~u(-t) \end{equation*}
