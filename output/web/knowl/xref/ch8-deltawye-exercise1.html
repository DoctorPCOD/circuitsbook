Convert the circuit shown in Figure 8.4.13 to a delta circuit.
🔗
Answer.
🔗
Solution.
Use (8.3.10), (8.3.11), and (8.3.12) to calculate the delta impedances.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_1} \amp= \frac{(j8~\Omega)(-j10~\Omega)+(-j10~\Omega)(30~\Omega)+(j8~\Omega)(30~\Omega)}{30~\Omega}\\ \amp= \frac{80-j60~\Omega^2}{30~\Omega}\\ \amp= 2.67-j2~\Omega\\ \vec{Z_2} \amp= \frac{80-j60~\Omega^2}{j8~\Omega}\\ \amp= -7.5-j10~\Omega\\ \vec{Z_3} \amp= \frac{80-j60~\Omega^2}{-j10~\Omega}\\ \amp= 6+j8~\Omega \end{align*}
