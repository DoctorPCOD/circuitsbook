Convert the circuit shown in Figure 8.4.15 to a wye circuit.
🔗
Answer.
🔗
Solution.
Use (8.3.7), (8.3.8), and (8.3.9) to calculate the wye impedances.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_a} \amp= \frac{(-j6.2~\Omega)(j13.4~\Omega)}{(-j6.2~\Omega)+(20-j3.9~\Omega)+(j13.4~\Omega)}\\ \amp= \frac{83.08~\Omega^2}{20+j3.3~\Omega}\\ \amp= 4.04-j0.67~\Omega\\ \vec{Z_b} \amp= \frac{(-j6.2~\Omega)(20-j3.9~\Omega)}{20+j3.3~\Omega}\\ \amp= -2.17-j5.84~\Omega\\ \vec{Z_c} \amp= \frac{(20-j3.9~\Omega)(j13.4~\Omega)}{20+j3.3~\Omega}\\ \amp= 4.70+j12.6~\Omega \end{align*}
