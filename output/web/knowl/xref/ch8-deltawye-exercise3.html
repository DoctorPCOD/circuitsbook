Exercise 13.

Calculate the equivalent impedance between nodes a and b of the circuit shown in Exercise 8.4.13.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} 2.86 - j2.17~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
A wye to delta transform will be done to solve this circuit. (A delta to wye transform can also be used. Feel free to try it out for extra practice.) The wye impedances are highlighted in red below, with the wye nodes labeled.
🔗
Use (8.3.10), (8.3.11), and (8.3.12) to calculate the delta impedances.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_1} \amp= \frac{(4~\Omega)(-j3~\Omega)+(-j3~\Omega)(j5~\Omega)+(4~\Omega)(j5~\Omega)}{j5~\Omega}\\ \amp= \frac{15 + j8~\Omega}{j5~\Omega}\\ \amp= 1.6 - j3~\Omega\\ \vec{Z_2} \amp= \frac{15 + j8~\Omega}{4~\Omega}\\ \amp= 3.75 + j2~\Omega\\ \vec{Z_3} \amp= \frac{15 + j8~\Omega}{-j3~\Omega}\\ \amp= -2.6667 + j5~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
The 9 Ω resistor is connected in parallel with the 3.75+j2 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z'} \amp= \frac{(9~\Omega)(3.75 + j2~\Omega)}{(9~\Omega)+(3.75 + j2~\Omega)}\\ \amp= 2.7996 + j0.9726~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
The 6 Ω resistor is connected in parallel with the −2.67+j5 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z''} \amp= \frac{(6~\Omega)(-2.6667 + j5~\Omega)}{(6~\Omega)+(-2.6667 + j5~\Omega)}\\ \amp= 2.6769 + j4.9846~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
The 2.68+j4.98 Ω impedance is connected in series with the 2.8+j0.973 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z'''} \amp= (2.7996 + j0.9726~\Omega) + (2.6769 + j4.9846~\Omega)\\ \amp= 5.4765 + j5.9572~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
The 1.6−j3 Ω impedance is connected in parallel with the 5.48+j5.96 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{EQ}} \amp= \frac{(5.4765 + j5.9572~\Omega)(1.6 - j3~\Omega)}{(5.4765 + j5.9572~\Omega)+(1.6 - j3~\Omega)}\\ \amp= 2.86 - j2.17~\Omega \end{align*}
