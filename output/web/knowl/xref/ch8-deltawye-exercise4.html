Exercise 14.

Calculate the equivalent impedance between nodes a and b of the circuit shown in Figure 8.4.22. The frequency of operation is 10 kHz.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} 149 - j134~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use (8.2.4) to calculate the impedance of the capacitor.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{1}{j(2\pi 10000)(100 \times 10^{-9})} = -j159.15~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Use (8.2.5) to calculate the impedance of the inductor.
🔗
\begin{equation*} j(2\pi 1000)(0.005) = j314.16~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
A wye to delta transform will be done to solve this circuit. (A delta to wye transform can also be used. Feel free to try it out for extra practice.) The wye impedances are highlighted in red below, with the wye nodes labeled.
🔗
Use (8.3.10), (8.3.11), and (8.3.12) to calculate the delta impedances.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_1} \amp= \frac{(150~\Omega)(-j159~\Omega)+(150~\Omega)(j314~\Omega)+(-j159~\Omega)(j314~\Omega)}{j314~\Omega}\\ \amp= \frac{50000 + j23251~\Omega}{j314~\Omega}\\ \amp= 74.009 - j159.15~\Omega\\ \vec{Z_2} \amp= \frac{50000 + j23251~\Omega}{-j159~\Omega}\\ \amp= -146.09 + j314.16~\Omega\\ \vec{Z_3} \amp= \frac{50000 + j23251~\Omega}{}\\ \amp= 333.33 + j155~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
The 220 Ω resistor is connected in parallel with the 333.33+j155 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{(220~\Omega)(333.33 + j155~\Omega)}{(220~\Omega)+(333.33 + j155~\Omega)} = 138.89 + j22.720~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
The 470 Ω resistor is connected in parallel with the −146.09+j314.16 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{(470~\Omega)(-146.09 + j314.16~\Omega)}{(470~\Omega)+(-146.09 + j314.16~\Omega)} = 118.59 + j340.83~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
The 139+j22.7 Ω impedance is connected in series with the 119+j341 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{equation*} (138.89 + j22.720~\Omega) + (118.59 + j340.83~\Omega) = 257.49 + j363.55~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
The 257+j364 Ω impedance is connected in parallel with the 74−j159 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{EQ}} \amp= \frac{(257.49 + j363.55~\Omega)(74.009 - j159.15~\Omega)}{(257.49 + j363.55~\Omega)+(74.009 - j159.15~\Omega)}\\ \amp= 149 - j134~\Omega \end{align*}
