Exercise 8.

Calculate the equivalent impedance between nodes a and b of the circuit shown in Figure 8.4.1. The frequency of operation is 200 Hz.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} 103 + j 16.1~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use (8.2.5) to calculate the impedance of the inductor.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_L} \amp= j(2\pi 200)(0.085)\\ \amp= j106.81~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
The inductor and 45 Ω resistor are connected in parallel.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{LR}} \amp= \frac{(45~\Omega)(j106.81~\Omega)}{45~\Omega + j106.81~\Omega}\\ \amp= 38.2170 + j16.1005~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Calculate the equivalent impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{EQ}} \amp= 38.22+j16.1~\Omega + 65~\Omega\\ \amp= 103 + j 16.1~\Omega \end{align*}
