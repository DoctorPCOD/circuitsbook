Exercise 9.

Calculate the equivalent impedance between nodes a and b of the circuit shown in Figure 8.4.4. The frequency of operation is 450 Hz.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} 24.0+j283~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use (8.2.4) to calculate the impedance of the capacitor.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_C} \amp= \frac{1}{j(2\pi450)(330\times10^{-9})}\\ \amp= -j1071.75~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use (8.2.5) to calculate the impedance of each inductor.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{L100}} \amp= j(2\pi 450)(0.1)\\ \amp= j282.74~\Omega\\ \vec{Z_{L460}} \amp= j(2\pi 450)(0.46)\\ \amp= j1300.62~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
The capacitor (−j1071.75 Ω impedance) and 24 Ω resistor are connected in parallel.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{RC}} \amp= \frac{(24~\Omega)(-j1071.75~\Omega)}{24~\Omega + (-j1071.75~\Omega)}\\ \amp= 23.9880 - j0.5372 \end{align*}
🔗
The 460 mH inductor (j1300.62 Ω impedance) and 86 Ω resistor are connected in series.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{LR}} \amp= 86~\Omega + j1300.62~\Omega\\ \amp= 86 + j1300.62~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
The 23.988−j0.5372 Ω impedance is in parallel with the 86+j1300.62 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z'} \amp= \frac{(23.9880 - j0.5372)(86 + j1300.62~\Omega)}{(23.9880 - j0.5372)+(86 + j1300.62~\Omega)}\\ \amp= 23.9705 - j0.0963 \end{align*}
🔗
The j282.74 Ω and 23.9705−j0.0963 Ω impedances are connected in series.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{EQ}} \amp= (23.97-j0.0963~\Omega) + (j282.74~\Omega)\\ \amp= 24.0+j283~\Omega \end{align*}
