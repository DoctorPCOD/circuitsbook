Exercise 10.

Calculate the equivalent impedance between nodes a and b of the circuit shown in Figure 8.4.8. The frequency of operation is 6 kHz.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} 0.0586 - j0.528~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use (8.2.5) to calculate the impedance of the inductor.
🔗
\begin{equation*} j(2\pi6000)(33\times10^{-6}) = j1.24~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Use (8.2.4) to calculate the impedance of each capacitor.
🔗
\begin{align*} \frac{1}{j(2\pi 6000)(50\times10^{-6})} \amp= -j0.53~\Omega\\ \frac{1}{j(2\pi 6000)(68\times10^{-6})} \amp= -j0.39~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
The 5 Ω resistor is connected in series with the j1.24 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{equation*} (5~\Omega) + (j1.24~\Omega) = 5 + j1.24~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
The 6 Ω resistor is connected in series with the −j0.39 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{equation*} (6~\Omega) + (-j0.39~\Omega) = 6 - j0.39~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
The 5+j1.24 Ω impedance is connected in parallel with the 6−j0.39 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{(5 + j1.24~\Omega)(6 - j0.39~\Omega)}{(5 + j1.24~\Omega)+(6 - j0.39~\Omega)} = 2.7935 + j0.2844 \end{equation*}
🔗
The 2 Ω resistor is connected in series with the 2.8+j0.28 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{equation*} 2~\Omega + (2.7935 + j0.2844) = 4.7935 + j0.2844 \end{equation*}
🔗
The −j0.53 Ω impedance is connected in parallel with the 4.8+j0.28 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{EQ}} \amp= \frac{(-j0.53~\Omega)(4.7935 + j0.2844)}{(-j0.53~\Omega)+(4.7935 + j0.2844)}\\ \amp= 0.0586 - j0.528~\Omega \end{align*}
