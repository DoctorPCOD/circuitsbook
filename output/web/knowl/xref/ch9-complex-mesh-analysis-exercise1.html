Exercise 11.

Use mesh analysis to calculate \(v(t)\) given the circuit diagram shown in Figure 9.6.26. The source frequency is 50 Hz.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} v(t) = 50.5~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi50t + 17.9 \pi/180) \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Mesh current \(\vec{I_A}\) will flow clockwise around the left mesh. Mesh current \(\vec{I_B}\) will flow clockwise around the right mesh. Derive the mesh equations.
🔗
\begin{align*} 105 \amp= 12\vec{I_A} + 24(\vec{I_A}-\vec{I_B})\\ 0 \amp= 24(\vec{I_B}-\vec{I_A}) + (9+j12)\vec{I_B} \end{align*}
🔗
The two equations can be expressed in a matrix. The left column contains coefficients of \(\vec{I_A}\text{,}\) the middle column contains coefficients of \(\vec{I_B}\text{,}\) and the right column contains constants.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \begin{bmatrix} 36 \amp -24 \amp 105 \\ -24 \amp 33+j12 \amp 0 \end{bmatrix} \end{equation*}
🔗
The matrix is shown in reduced row echelon form below.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \begin{bmatrix} 1 \amp 0 \amp 4.75-j1.29\\ 0 \amp 1 \amp 2.75-j1.94\\ \end{bmatrix} \end{equation*}
🔗
Use Ohm’s law to calculate \(\vec{V}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V} \amp= (24~\Omega)(\vec{I_A}-\vec{I_B})\\ \amp= (24~\Omega)((4.75-j1.29~\textrm{A})-(2.75-j1.94~\textrm{A}))\\ \amp= 48.01+j15.52~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 50.46~\textrm{V} \angle 17.91^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Convert to time-varying form.
🔗
\begin{equation*} v(t) = 50.5~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi50t + 17.9 \pi/180) \end{equation*}
