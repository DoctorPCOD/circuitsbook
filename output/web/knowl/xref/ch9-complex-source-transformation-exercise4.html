Exercise 24.

Use source transformation to calculate \(v(t)\) for the circuit shown in Figure 9.6.79. The current source has a value of \(i_{AC}(t) = 3~\textrm{A}~\cos(2\pi50000t)\text{.}\) The voltage source has a value of \(v_{AC}(t) = 15~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi50000t)\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} v(t) = 29.8~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi50000t + 3.86\pi/180) \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use (8.2.4) to calculate the impedance of the capacitor.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_C} \amp= \frac{1}{j(2\pi50000)(500 \times 10^{-9})}\\ \amp= -j6.3662~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use (8.2.5) to calculate the impedance of the inductor.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_L} \amp= j(2\pi50000)(25 \times 10^{-6})\\ \amp= j7.8540~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Convert both sources to phasors.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{I_{AC}} \amp= 3~\cos{0^\circ} +j3~\sin{0^\circ}~\textrm{A}\\ \amp= 3~\textrm{A}\\ \vec{V_{AC}} \amp= 15~\cos{0^\circ} +j15~\sin{0^\circ}~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 15~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Convert the current source into a voltage source. Convert the voltage source into a current source.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{I_S} \amp= \frac{15~\textrm{V}}{9~\Omega}\\ \amp= 1.67~\textrm{A}\\ \vec{V_S} \amp= (3~\textrm{A})(j7.8540~\Omega)~\Omega\\ \amp= j23.56~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
The 12 Ω resistor is connected in parallel with the 9 Ω resistor.
🔗
Convert the current source to a voltage source.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_{S2}} \amp= (1.67~\textrm{A})(5.14~\Omega)\\ \amp= 8.57~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Combine the two voltage sources.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_{TOTAL}} \amp= (j23.56~\textrm{V}) - (8.57~\textrm{V})\\ \amp= -8.5714 + j23.5619~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(\vec{V}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V} \amp= (-8.57+j23.56~\textrm{V})\left(\frac{-j6.37~\Omega}{(-j6.37~\Omega)+(5.14+j7.85~\Omega)}\right)\\ \amp= 29.7467 + 2.0049~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 29.8141~\textrm{V} \angle 3.8558^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Convert to time-varying form.
🔗
\begin{equation*} v(t) = 29.8~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi50000t + 3.86\pi/180) \end{equation*}
