Exercise 17.

Calculate \(\vec{V}\) given the circuit diagram shown in Figure 9.6.40.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} -5.16 + j12.0~\textrm{V} \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Calculate \(\vec{V_1}\text{,}\) the voltage contributed by the 5 V source. Deactivate the 30 V source.
🔗
The j15 Ω impedance is connected in parallel with the −j40 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{(j15~\Omega)(-j40~\Omega)}{(j15~\Omega)+(-j40~\Omega)} = j24~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(\vec{V_1}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_1} \amp= (5~\textrm{V} \angle 15^\circ)\left(\frac{j24~\Omega}{j24~\Omega+20~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= 2.2138 + j3.1390~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Calculate \(\vec{V_2}\text{,}\) the voltage contributed by the 30 V source. Deactivate the 5 V source.
🔗
The j15 Ω impedance is connected in parallel with the 20 Ω resistor.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{(j15~\Omega)(20~\Omega)}{(j15~\Omega)+(20~\Omega)} = 7.2+j9.6~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(\vec{V_2}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_2} \amp= (30~\textrm{V} \angle 0^\circ)\left(\frac{7.2+j9.6~\Omega}{-32.8+j9.6~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= -7.3770 + j8.8525~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Calculate \(\vec{V}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V} \amp= \vec{V_1}+\vec{V_2}\\ \amp= (2.21+j3.14~\textrm{V}) + (-7.38+j8.85~\textrm{V})\\ \amp= -5.16 + j12.0~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
