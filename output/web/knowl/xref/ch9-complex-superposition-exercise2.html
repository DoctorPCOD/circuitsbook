Exercise 16.

Calculate \(v(t)\) given the circuit diagram shown in Figure 9.6.36. The frequency of operation is 40 Hz.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} v(t) = 31.6~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi 40t - 71.6\pi/180) \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The circuit can be simplified before starting the superposition process. The 5 Ω resistor is connected in parallel with the j5 Ω impedance as well as with the −20/9 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z'} \amp= \frac{1}{\frac{1}{5~\Omega}+\frac{1}{-j20/9~\Omega}+\frac{1}{j5~\Omega}}\\ \amp= 1.9512 - j2.4390~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Calculate \(\vec{V_1}\text{,}\) the voltage contributed by the 10 A source. Deactivate the 100 V source.
🔗
Calculate the equivalent impedance of the circuit.
🔗
\begin{align*} Z_{EQ1} \amp= \frac{(1.9512 - j2.4390~\Omega)(20~\Omega)}{(1.9512 - j2.4390~\Omega)+(20~\Omega)}\\ \amp= 2-j2~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use Ohm’s law to calculate \(\vec{V_1}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_1} \amp= (10~\textrm{A})(2-j2~\Omega)\\ \amp= 20-j20~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Calculate \(\vec{V_2}\text{,}\) the voltage contributed by the 100 V source. Deactivate the 10 A source.
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(\vec{V_2}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_2} \amp= (-j100~\textrm{V})\left(\frac{1.9512 - j2.4390~\Omega}{21.95-j2.44~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= -10-j10~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Calculate \(\vec{V}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V} \amp= \vec{V_1}+\vec{V_2}\\ \amp= (20-j20~\textrm{V}) + (-10-j10~\textrm{V})\\ \amp= 10-j30~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 31.6228~\textrm{V} \angle -71.5651^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Convert to time-varying form.
🔗
\begin{equation*} v(t) = 31.6~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi 40t - 71.6\pi/180) \end{equation*}
