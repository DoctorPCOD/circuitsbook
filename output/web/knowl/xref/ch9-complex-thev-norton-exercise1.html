Exercise 26.

Derive the Thévenin equivalent circuit between nodes a and b in the circuit shown in Figure 9.6.91. The source value is \(v_{AC}(t) = 10~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi50t + 30\pi/180)\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
🔗
Solution.
Use (8.2.4) to calculate the impedance of the capacitor.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_C} \amp= \frac{1}{j(2\pi50)(750 \times 10^{-6})}\\ \amp= -j4.2441~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use (8.2.5) to calculate the impedance of the inductor.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_L} \amp= j(2\pi50)(0.025)\\ \amp= j7.8540~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Transform the source into a phasor.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_{AC}} \amp= 10~\cos{30^\circ} + j10~\sin{30^\circ}~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 8.6603 + j5~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(\vec{V_{TH}}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_{TH}} \amp= (8.66+j5~\textrm{V})\left(\frac{j7.85~\Omega}{5+j7.85~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= 3.90+j7.48~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 8.44~\textrm{V} \angle 62.48^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Deactivate the source and calculate the equivalent impedance of the circuit.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{TH}} \amp= (-j4.24~\Omega)+(5~\Omega)//(j7.85~\Omega)\\ \amp= (-j4.24~\Omega)+(3.56+j2.27~\Omega)\\ \amp= 3.56-j1.98~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use (8.2.4) to calculate the value of the capacitance based on the Thévenin equivalent reactance of the circuit.
🔗
\begin{align*} C \amp= -\frac{1}{2\pi 50 (-1.98)}\\ \amp= 1.61~\textrm{mF} \end{align*}
