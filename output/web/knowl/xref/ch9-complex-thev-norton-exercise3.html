Exercise 28.

Derive the Thévenin equivalent circuit between nodes a and b in the circuit shown in Figure 9.6.97. The voltage source has a value of \(v_{AC}(t) = 50~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi 2000t)\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
🔗
Solution.
Use (8.2.4) to calculate the impedance of the capacitor.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_C} \amp= \frac{1}{j(2\pi2000)(10 \times 10^{-6})}\\ \amp= -j7.9577~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use (8.2.5) to calculate the impedance of the inductors.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{L600}} \amp= j(2\pi 2000)(600\times10^{-6})\\ \amp= j7.5398~\Omega\\ \vec{Z_{L400}} \amp= j(2\pi 2000)(400\times10^{-6})\\ \amp= j5.0265~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Transform the source into a phasor.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_{AC}} \amp= 50~\cos{0^\circ} + j50~\sin{0^\circ}~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 50~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
The 10 Ω resistor is connected in series with the j7.54 Ω impedance. The 6 Ω resistor is connected in series with the j5.03 Ω impedance.
🔗
The −j7.96 Ω impedance is connected in parallel with the 6+j5.03 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z'} \amp= \frac{(-j7.9577~\Omega)(6+j5.0265~\Omega)}{(-j7.9577~\Omega)+(6+j5.0265~\Omega)}\\ \amp= 8.5207 - j3.7951~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(\vec{V_{TH}}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_{TH}} \amp= 50~\textrm{V}\left(\frac{8.52-j3.80~\Omega}{(10+j7.54~\Omega)+(8.52-j3.80~\Omega)}\right)\\ \amp= 20.11-j14.31~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 24.68~\textrm{V} \angle -35.44^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Deactivate the source and calculate the Thévenin equivalent impedance.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{TH}} \amp= (10+j7.54~\Omega)//(8.52-j3.80~\Omega)\\ \amp= 6.18+j0.17~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use (8.2.5) to calculate the value of the inductance based on the Thévenin equivalent reactance of the circuit.
🔗
\begin{align*} L \amp= \frac{0.17}{2 \pi 2000}\\ \amp= 13.54~\mu\textrm{H} \end{align*}
