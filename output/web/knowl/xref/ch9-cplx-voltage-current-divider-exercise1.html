Exercise 1.

Calculate \(v(t)\) given the circuit diagram shown in Figure 9.6.1. The source is equal to \(v_{AC}(t) = 50~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi60t + 60\pi/180)\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} v(t) = 41.7~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi60t + 26.6\pi/180) \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use (8.2.4) to calculate the impedance of the capacitor.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{1}{j(2\pi60)(35\times10^{-6})} = -j75.7881~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Convert the source to phasor form.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_{AC}} \amp= 50\cos(60^\circ)+j50\sin(60^\circ)~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 25 + j43.3013~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(\vec{V}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V} \amp= (25+j43.30~\textrm{V})\left(\frac{-j75.79~\Omega}{50~\Omega-j75.79~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= 37.3227 + j18.6782~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 41.7356~\textrm{V} \angle 26.5858^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Convert to time-varying form.
🔗
\begin{equation*} v(t) = 41.7~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi60t + 26.6\pi/180) \end{equation*}
