Exercise 2.

Calculate \(v(t)\) given the circuit diagram shown in Figure 9.6.3. The source is equal to \(v_{AC}(t) = 160~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi80t + 150\pi/180)\)
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} v(t) = 175~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi80t+140\pi/180) \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use (8.2.4) to calculate the impedance of the capacitor.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_C} \amp= \frac{1}{j(2\pi80)(26\times10^{-6})}\\ \amp= -j76.5168~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Use (8.2.5) to calculate the impedance of the inductor.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_L} \amp= j(2\pi80)(0.053)\\ \amp= j26.6407~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Convert the source to phasor form.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_{AC}} \amp= 160\cos(150^\circ)+j160\sin(150^\circ)~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= -138.56 + j80~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(\vec{V}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V} \amp= (-138.56+j80~\textrm{V})\left(\frac{120-j75.8~\Omega}{120-j75.8~\Omega+j26.4~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= -134.25+j112.26~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 175.2265~\textrm{V} \angle 140.0462^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Convert to time-varying form.
🔗
\begin{equation*} v(t) = 175~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi80t+140\pi/180) \end{equation*}
