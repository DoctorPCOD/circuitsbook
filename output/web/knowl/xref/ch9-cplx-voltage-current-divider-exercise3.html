Exercise 3.

Calculate \(i(t)\) given the circuit diagram shown in Figure 9.6.5. The source frequency is 150 Hz.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} i(t) = 22.1~\textrm{A}~\cos(2\pi150t+168\pi/180) \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The 6 Ω resistor is connected in series with the j5 Ω impedance.
🔗
Calculate the equivalent impedance of the circuit as seen by the source.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{Z_{EQ}} \amp= \frac{(-j7~\Omega)(6+j5~\Omega)}{(-j7~\Omega)+(6+j5~\Omega)}\\ \amp= 7.35 - j4.55~\Omega \end{align*}
🔗
Calculate the Cartesian form of the source.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{I_{AC}} \amp= 20\cos(-120^\circ)+j20\sin(-120^\circ)~\textrm{A}\\ \amp= -10 - j17.3205~\textrm{A} \end{align*}
🔗
Use the current divider rule.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{I} \amp= (-10 - j17.3205~\textrm{A})\left(\frac{7.35 - j4.55~\Omega}{6+j5~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= -21.6865 + j4.4378~\textrm{A}\\ \amp= 22.1359~\textrm{A} \angle 168.4349^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Convert to time-varying form.
🔗
\begin{equation*} i(t) = 22.1~\textrm{A}~\cos(2\pi150t+168\pi/180) \end{equation*}
