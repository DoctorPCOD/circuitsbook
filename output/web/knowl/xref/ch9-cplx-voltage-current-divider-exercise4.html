Exercise 4.

Calculate \(v(t)\) given the circuit diagram shown in Figure 9.6.7. The source voltage is \(v_{AC}(t) = 7~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi 100t)\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} v(t) = 3.23~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi100t-63.9\pi/180) \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use (8.2.4) to calculate the impedance of the capacitor.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{1}{j(2\pi100)(48\times10^{-6})} = -j33.1573~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Use (8.2.5) to calculate the impedance of the inductor.
🔗
\begin{equation*} j(2\pi100)(0.052) = j32.6726~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Convert the source to phasor form.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V_{AC}} \amp= 7\cos(0^\circ)+j7\sin(0^\circ)~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 7~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
🔗
The 28 Ω resistor is connected in series with the j32.7 Ω impedance. The 30 Ω resistor is connected in parallel with the −j33.2 Ω impedance.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{(30~\Omega)(-j33.1573~\Omega)}{(30~\Omega)+(-j33.1573~\Omega)} = 16.4960 - j14.9252~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{V} \amp= 7~\textrm{V}\left(\frac{16.50-j14.93~\Omega}{16.50-j14.93~\Omega+28+j32.67~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= 1.43 - j2.92~\textrm{V}\\ \amp= 3.2506~\textrm{V} \angle -63.8828^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Convert to time-varying form.
🔗
\begin{equation*} v(t) = 3.23~\textrm{V}~\cos(2\pi100t-63.9\pi/180) \end{equation*}
