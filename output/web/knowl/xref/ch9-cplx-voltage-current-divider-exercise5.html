Exercise 5.

Calculate \(i(t)\) given the circuit diagram shown in Figure 9.6.10. The source value is \(i_{AC}(t) = 4.5~\textrm{A}~\cos(2\pi6000t + 30\pi/180)\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} i(t) = 3.92~\textrm{A}~\cos(2\pi6000t + 59.4\pi/180) \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use (8.2.4) to calculate the impedance of the capacitor.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{1}{j(2\pi6000)(330\times10^{-9})} = -j80.3813~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Use (8.2.5) to calculate the impedance of the inductor.
🔗
\begin{equation*} j(2\pi6000)(0.001) = j37.6991~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Convert the source to phasor form.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{I_{AC}} \amp= 4.5\cos(30^\circ)+j4.5\sin(30^\circ)~\textrm{A}\\ \amp= 3.8971 + j2.25~\textrm{A} \end{align*}
🔗
Calculate the equivalent impedance of the circuit as seen by the source.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \frac{1}{\frac{1}{40~\Omega}+\frac{1}{j37.6991~\Omega}+\frac{1}{-j80.3813~\Omega}} = 30.3623 + j17.1062~\Omega \end{equation*}
🔗
Apply the current divider rule.
🔗
\begin{align*} \vec{I} \amp= (3.90+j2.25~\textrm{A})\left(\frac{30.3623 + j17.1062}{40~\Omega}\right)\\ \amp= 1.9959 + j3.3745~\textrm{A}\\ \amp= 3.9206~\textrm{A} \angle 59.3971^\circ \end{align*}
🔗
Convert to time-varying form.
🔗
\begin{equation*} i(t) = 3.92~\textrm{A}~\cos(2\pi6000t + 59.4\pi/180) \end{equation*}
