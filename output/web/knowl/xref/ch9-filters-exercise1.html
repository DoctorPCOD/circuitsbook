Exercise 36.

Given the circuit shown in Figure 9.6.125, determine the filter type. Then, calculate the quality factor of the filter.
🔗
Answer.
The circuit is a passive, second-order, low-pass filter.
🔗
\begin{equation*} Q = \frac{\sqrt{L}}{R\sqrt{C}} \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Analyze the circuit at \(\omega=0\text{.}\) \(v_{out}(0)\) is not zero.
🔗
Analyze the circuit at \(\omega=0\text{.}\) \(v_{out}(\infty)\) is zero.
🔗
This circuit is a low-pass filter. (Refer to Table 9.3.31.)
🔗
This is a series RLC circuit with a homogeneous differential equation given by (7.3.2). The resonant frequency is equal to
🔗
\begin{equation*} \omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}\text{.} \end{equation*}
🔗
Use (9.3.2) to calculate the quality factor.
🔗
\begin{align*} Q \amp= \frac{\frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}}{\frac{R}{L}}\\ \amp= \frac{L}{R\sqrt{LC}}\\ \amp= \frac{\sqrt{L}}{R\sqrt{C}} \end{align*}
