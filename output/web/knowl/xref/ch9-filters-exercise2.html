Exercise 37.

Given the circuit shown in Figure 9.6.128, determine the filter type. Then, calculate the cutoff frequency and quality factor of the circuit. The circuit parameters are \(L = 0.1~\textrm{H}\text{,}\) \(R = 100~\Omega\text{,}\) and \(C=40~\mu\textrm{F}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
The circuit is a passive, second-order, high-pass filter.
🔗
\begin{align*} \omega_c \amp= 337~\textrm{rad/s}\\ Q \amp= 2 \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Analyze the circuit at \(\omega=0\text{.}\) \(v_{out}(0)\) is zero.
🔗
Analyze the circuit at \(\omega \rightarrow \infty\text{.}\) \(v_{out}(\infty)\) is not zero.
🔗
This circuit is a high-pass filter. (Refer to Table 9.3.31.)
🔗
This is a parallel RLC circuit with a homogeneous differential equation given by (7.4.4).
🔗
The transfer function of this circuit is
🔗
\begin{equation*} H(j\omega) = \frac{-\omega^2}{-\omega^2 + \frac{1}{RC}j\omega + \frac{1}{LC}}\text{.} \end{equation*}
🔗
Plug in parameter values using base units.
🔗
\begin{equation*} H(j\omega) = \frac{-\omega^2}{-\omega^2 + 250j\omega + 250000} \end{equation*}
🔗
Use (7.4.6) to alculate the resonant frequency of the circuit.
🔗
\begin{align*} \omega_0 \amp= \frac{1}{\sqrt{(0.1)(40 \times 10^{-6})}}\\ \amp= 500~\textrm{rad/s} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (9.3.2) to calculate the quality factor.
🔗
\begin{align*} Q \amp= \frac{500}{250}\\ \amp= 2 \end{align*}
🔗
Set \(|H(j\omega)|\) equal to \(1/\sqrt{2}\) and solve for \(\omega\) to calculate the cutoff frequency of the circuit.
🔗
\begin{align*} |H(j\omega_c)| = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \amp= \frac{-\omega_c^2}{\sqrt{(a_0-\omega_c^2)^2 + (a_1\omega_c)^2}}\\ 2(\omega_c^2)^2 \amp= (a_0-\omega_c^2)^2 + (a_1\omega_c)^2\\ \amp= a_1^2\omega_c^2 + (a_0-\omega_c^2)^2 \end{align*}
🔗
Let \(x = \omega_c^2\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} 2x^2 \amp= a_1^2x + (a_0-x)^2\\ \amp= a_1^2x + a_0^2 - 2a_0x + x^2\\ 0 \amp= x^2 + (2a_0 - a_1^2)x - a_0^2\\ \amp= x^2 + 437500 x - (250000)^2\\ \omega_c \amp= 337~\textrm{rad/s} \end{align*}
