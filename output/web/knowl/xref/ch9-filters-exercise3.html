Exercise 38.

Given the circuit shown in Figure 9.6.131, determine the filter type. Then, calculate the center frequency, bandwidth, cutoff frequencies, and quality factor.
🔗
Answer.
The circuit is a passive, second-order, band-stop filter.
🔗
\begin{align*} \omega_c \amp= 100000~\textrm{rad/s}\\ \beta \amp= 100000~\textrm{rad/s}\\ \omega_{c1} \amp= 50000~\textrm{rad/s}\\ \omega_{c2} \amp= 150000~\textrm{rad/s}\\ Q \amp= 1 \end{align*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Analyze the circuit at \(\omega=0\text{.}\) \(v_{out}(0)\) is not zero.
🔗
Analyze the circuit at \(\omega \rightarrow \infty\text{.}\) \(v_{out}(\infty)\) is not zero.
🔗
This circuit is a band-stop filter. (Refer to Table 9.3.31.)
🔗
The transfer function of this circuit is
🔗
\begin{equation*} H(j\omega) = \frac{-\omega^2 + \frac{1}{LC}}{\omega^2 + \frac{1}{RC}j\omega + \frac{1}{LC}}\text{.} \end{equation*}
🔗
Plug in parameter values using base units.
🔗
\begin{equation*} H(j\omega) = \frac{-\omega^2 + 1 \times 10^{10}}{\omega^2 + 100000j\omega + 1 \times 10^{10}} \end{equation*}
🔗
Use (9.3.1) to calculate the center frequency.
🔗
\begin{align*} \omega_c \amp= \sqrt{1\times10^{10}}\\ \amp= 100000~\textrm{rad/s} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (9.3.4) to calculate the bandwidth.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \beta = 100000~\textrm{rad/s} \end{equation*}
🔗
The two cutoff frequencies can be calculated directly from the center frequency and bandwidth.
🔗
\begin{align*} \omega_{c1}, \omega_{c2} \amp= \omega_0 \pm \frac{\beta}{2}\\ \omega_{c1} \amp= \omega_0 - \frac{\beta}{2}\\ \amp= 100000~\textrm{rad/s} - 50000~\textrm{rad/s}\\ \amp= 50000~\textrm{rad/s}\\ \omega_{c2} \amp= \omega_0 + \frac{\beta}{2}\\ \amp= 100000~\textrm{rad/s} + 5000~\textrm{rad/s}\\ \amp= 150000~\textrm{rad/s} \end{align*}
🔗
Use (9.3.2) to calculate the quality factor.
🔗
\begin{align*} Q \amp= \frac{100000}{100000}\\ \amp= 1 \end{align*}
