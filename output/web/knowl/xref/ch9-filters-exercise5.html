Exercise 40.

Given the circuit shown in Figure 9.6.137, determine the filter type and calculate the cutoff frequency. The component values are \(R_1 = 30~\textrm{k}\Omega\text{,}\) \(R_2 = 20~\textrm{k}\Omega\text{,}\) and \(C = 5~\mu\textrm{F}\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
The circuit is an active, first-order, low-pass filter.
🔗
\begin{equation*} \omega_c = 10~\textrm{rad/s} \end{equation*}
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Analyze the circuit at \(\omega=0\text{.}\) \(v_{out}(0)\) is not zero.
🔗
Analyze the circuit at \(\omega \rightarrow \infty\text{.}\) \(v_{out}(\infty)\) is zero.
🔗
This circuit is a low-pass filter. (Refer to Table 9.3.31.)
🔗
Derive the differential equation (in standard form) corresponding to the circuit.
🔗
\begin{equation*} -\frac{1}{R_1C} = \frac{d}{dt}v_{out}(t) + \frac{1}{R_2C}v_{out}(t) \end{equation*}
🔗
The transfer function of this circuit is
🔗
\begin{equation*} H(j\omega) = \frac{-\frac{1}{R_1C}}{j\omega + \frac{1}{R_2C}}\text{.} \end{equation*}
🔗
The gain of the op-amp circuit at \(\omega = 0\) is equal to
🔗
\begin{equation*} G = -\frac{R_2}{R_1}\text{.} \end{equation*}
🔗
Set \(|H(j\omega)|\) equal to \(1/\sqrt{2}\text{.}\) Divide by the op-amp gain. Then, solve for \(\omega\) to calculate the cutoff frequency of the circuit.
🔗
\begin{align*} \frac{|H(j\omega_c)|}{G} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} \amp= \Big|\frac{-\frac{1}{R_1C}}{(-\frac{R_2}{R_1})(j\omega_c + \frac{1}{R_2C})}\Big|\\ \amp= \Big|\frac{\frac{1}{R_2C}}{j\omega_c + \frac{1}{R_2C}}\Big|\\ \amp= \frac{\frac{1}{R_2C}}{\sqrt{\omega_c^2 + \left(\frac{1}{R_2C}\right)^2}}\\ \frac{1}{2} \amp= \frac{\left(\frac{1}{R_2C}\right)^2}{\omega_c^2 + \left(\frac{1}{R_2C}\right)^2}\\ \omega_c^2 + \left(\frac{1}{R_2C}\right)^2 \amp= 2\left(\frac{1}{R_2C}\right)^2\\ \omega_c^2 \amp= \left(\frac{1}{R_2C}\right)^2\\ \omega_c \amp= \frac{1}{R_2C}\\ \amp= \frac{1}{(20000)(5 \times 10^{-6})}\\ \amp= 10~\textrm{rad/s} \end{align*}
