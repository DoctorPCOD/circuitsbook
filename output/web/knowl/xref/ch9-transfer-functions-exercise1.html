Exercise 31.

Derive a transfer function for the circuit shown in Figure 9.6.110. Then, determine the filter type.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} H(j\omega) = \frac{\frac{1}{CR_1}}{j\omega + \frac{R_1+R_2}{CR_1R_2}} \end{equation*}
🔗
The circuit is a passive, first-order, low-pass filter.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Calculate the equivalent impedance of \(R_2\) in parallel with \(C\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} R_2//C \amp= R_2//\frac{1}{j\omega C}\\ \amp= \frac{\frac{R_2}{j\omega C}}{R_2 + \frac{1}{j\omega C}}\\ \amp= \frac{R_2}{j\omega CR_2 + 1} \end{align*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(H(j\omega)\) and reduce to standard form.
🔗
\begin{align*} H(j\omega) \amp= \frac{\frac{R_2}{j\omega CR_2 + 1}}{R_1+\frac{R_2}{j\omega CR_2 + 1}}\\ \amp= \frac{R_2}{j\omega CR_2R_1 + R_1 + R_2}\\ \amp= \frac{\frac{1}{CR_1}}{j\omega + \frac{R_1+R_2}{CR_1R_2}} \end{align*}
🔗
Analyze the circuit at \(\omega=0\text{.}\) \(v_{out}(0)\) is not zero.
🔗
Analyze the circuit at \(\omega \rightarrow \infty\text{.}\) \(v_{out}(\infty)\) is zero.
🔗
This circuit is a passive, first-order, low-pass filter. (Refer to Table 9.3.31.)
