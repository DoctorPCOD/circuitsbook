Exercise 33.

Derive a transfer function for the circuit shown in Figure 9.6.116. Then, determine the filter type.
🔗
Answer.
\begin{equation*} H(j\omega) = \frac{-\omega^2 + j\omega \frac{1}{RC}}{-\omega^2 + j\omega\frac{1}{RC} + \frac{1}{LC}} \end{equation*}
🔗
The circuit is a passive, second-order, high-pass filter.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Calculate the equivalent impedance of the resistor and capacitor in parallel.
🔗
\begin{align*} R//C \amp= R//\frac{1}{j\omega C}\\ \amp= \frac{\frac{R}{j\omega C}}{R + \frac{1}{j\omega C}}\\ \amp= \frac{R}{j\omega CR + 1} \end{align*}
🔗
Use the voltage divider rule to calculate \(H(j\omega)\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} H(j\omega) \amp= \frac{j\omega L}{j\omega L + \frac{R}{j\omega CR + 1}}\\ \amp= \frac{-\omega^2 LCR + j\omega L}{-\omega^2 LCR + j\omega L + R}\\ \amp= \frac{-\omega^2 + j\omega \frac{1}{RC}}{-\omega^2 + j\omega\frac{1}{RC} + \frac{1}{LC}} \end{align*}
🔗
Analyze the circuit at \(\omega=0\text{.}\) \(v_{out}(0)\) is zero.
🔗
Analyze the circuit at \(\omega \rightarrow \infty\text{.}\) \(v_{out}(\infty)\) is not zero.
🔗
This circuit is a passive, second-order, high-pass filter. (Refer to Table 9.3.31.)
