Exercise 18.

Sketch the function \(f(t) = 3~\textrm{tri}(t+2) -2~\textrm{tri}\left(\frac{t-5}{5}\right)\text{.}\)
🔗
Answer.
🔗
Solution.
The first term in the expression is a triangle function centered at \(t = -2~\textrm{s}\) with a half-width of \(\tau = 1~\textrm{s}\) and an amplitude of \(A = 3\text{.}\)
🔗
The second term in the expression is a triangle function centered at \(t = 5~\textrm{s}\) with a half-width of \(\tau = 5~\textrm{s}\) and an amplitude of \(A = -2\text{.}\)
