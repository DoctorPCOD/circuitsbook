Derivation

Ohm’s law is used to calculate \(I_{LOAD}\) and the voltage divider rule is used to calculate \(V_{LOAD}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} I_{LOAD} \amp= \frac{V_{TH}}{R_{TH}+R_{LOAD}}\\ V_{LOAD} \amp= V_{TH} \left( \frac{R_{LOAD}}{R_{TH}+R_{LOAD}} \right)\\ P_{LOAD} \amp= I_{LOAD}V_{LOAD}\\ \amp= \frac{R_{LOAD}V_{TH}^2}{(R_{TH}+R_{LOAD})^2} \end{align*}
