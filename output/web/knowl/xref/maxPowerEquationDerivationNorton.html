Derivation

Ohm’s law is used to calculate \(V_{LOAD}\) and the current divider rule is used to calculate \(I_{LOAD}\text{.}\)
🔗
\begin{align*} I_{LOAD} \amp= \frac{I_NR_N}{R_N+R_{LOAD}}\\ V_{LOAD} \amp= I_N \left( \frac{R_{LOAD}R_N}{R_N+R_{LOAD}} \right)\\ P_{LOAD} \amp= I_{LOAD}V_{LOAD}\\ \amp= \frac{I_N^2R_N^2R_{LOAD}}{(R_N+R_{LOAD})^2} \end{align*}
