Exercise 1.

How much power is supplied by the unknown element shown in Figure 1.7.1?
🔗
Answer.
P = 8 mW
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Use conservation of power to calculate the power consumed by the unknown circuit element.
🔗
\begin{align*} 10~\textrm{mW} + 3~\textrm{mW} -5~\textrm{mW} + P \amp = 0\\ P \amp = -8~\textrm{mW} \end{align*}
🔗
The negative sign indicates that the circuit element supplies 8 mW of power (hence the positive sign in the answer).
