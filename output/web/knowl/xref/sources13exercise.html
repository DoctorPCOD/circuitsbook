Exercise 13.

Calculate the current supplied by the dependent source as depicted in Figure 1.7.7.
🔗
Hint.
Refer to Table 1.5.6 and Subsection 1.3.2.
🔗
In particular, note if the ammeter’s measured current is in the same or the opposite direction as \(I_X\text{.}\)
🔗
🔗
Answer.
\(I = -0.96~\textrm{mA}\)
🔗
🔗
Solution.
The ammeter reads a value of 4.8 mA, which means that the current \(I_X\) must be equal to −4.8 mA because the ammeter is in series with the current \(I_X\text{.}\) The negative sign comes from the fact that the + sign of the ammeter does not correspond to the direction of the current \(I_X\text{.}\) (The negative end of the ammeter is implied to be at the “bottom” of the diagram, which means that \(I_X\) flows from minus to plus through the ammeter.)
🔗
Use the relationship of a CCCS defined in Table 1.5.6 to determine the value of the source current.
🔗
\begin{align*} I_S \amp = 0.2I_X\\ \amp = 0.2(-4.8~\textrm{mA})\\ \amp = -0.96~\textrm{mA} \end{align*}
