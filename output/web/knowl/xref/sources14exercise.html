Exercise 14.

Reduce the circuit shown in Figure 1.7.8 to a circuit that contains only one independent source.
🔗
Answer.
🔗
Solution.
The two voltage sources can be added because they are in series with each other. The voltage of the combined source is calculated below. The two sources add because their polarities are in the same direction.
🔗
\begin{align*} V \amp = 3~\textrm{V} + 4~\textrm{V}\\ \amp = 7~\textrm{V} \end{align*}
