Exercise 15.

Reduce the circuit shown in Figure 1.7.10 as much as possible. Assume that no voltages need to be calculated or measured in between sources. The unit of the proportionality constant (\(r_m\)) on the dependent source is V/mA.
🔗
Answer.
🔗
Solution.
The 10 V independent source can be combined with the dependent source because they are both in series with each other. The dependent source has the opposite polarity to the independent source, so it should be subtracted to calculate the total voltage of the combined sources. The unit of the proportionality constant is in V/mA, so no unit conversion needs to be done prior to the subtraction operation (any value of \(I_X\) used to calculate the voltage must have a unit of mA).
🔗
\begin{equation*} V = 10~\textrm{V} - 0.4I_X \end{equation*}
🔗
The 8 V source cannot be combined with the other two sources because it is not in series with them.
